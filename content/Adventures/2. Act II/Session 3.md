	- JPL New Press working as intended, workers are secretive and scared. Avoiding the place, some decided to resign.
	- Old Press is in lockdown, at night animated gargoyles are printing *something*.
	- Administration holds meetings with Judgement Priests.

		- Left wing housed women, currently mostly empty.
