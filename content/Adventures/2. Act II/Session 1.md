## **Characters**
**Garrim** - priest of the Hemp Rope cleansing the Domain of Death
**Stará Zverina** - conscientious healer forcefully pulled into godhood
**Porcelain Lady** - sophisticated automaton wizard with a sense for art and sorcery
## **Scenes**
- An informer has sent a message that he found something interesting but disappeared. Zverina arrives and Garrim shares his findings. The tavern serves new meat and customers get poisoned. The innkeep got the meat from a poacher.
- The poacher is hiding in a grotto, where Porcelain Lady stands frozen, an idol of faith for villagers. The poacher accuses party of being "one of them" who worship the Lord of Death. A strange magical mechanism forces the party to bring Lady back to life.
- Informant Balmil has notes: JPL suspicious, shipments in docks, disappearings, red and yellow masked people, "Dakhumin Homebrew was right - the White Sorceress knows something, I have to hear her out"
## **Secrets and clues**
- A kid disappeared in the lands around the village. One of the beasts that escaped from JPL are at fault.
- The informant went missing few days ago, just after sending the message. A crew of Syndicate remnants hired to clean up the mess kidnapped him.
- The eccentric journalist collects bizzare animals from around the world.
- Journalist's main exhibition seems to be in dire state, the mansion abandoned and animals nowhere to be found.
	- Kids in the village are playing with a very rare herbivore.
- The R'aulek Maw with Porcelain Lady's face processes food deep below the mansion.
- Father Maxim thrown fisherman Rufin into servitude after Rufin openly spoke against him during sermon.
- Below the church is a device placed there by Maxim and Church that redirects the townsfolk prayers to Crassus.
- The looming threat of war made ink oil more expensive, JPL struggles.
- The JPL Old Press building is in lockdown and workers are avoiding the place, hearing strange sounds. Some of them decided to resign.
- Zverina is being involuntarily possesed by a god. The options are: "change her ways", "give up any connection to magic", "sever connection using ritual".
