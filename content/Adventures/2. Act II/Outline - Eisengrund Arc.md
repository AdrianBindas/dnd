## **Collisions**
- The construction of Hell Machine is closing in, only few steps remain. The Power Core is missing. A Relay Transmitter is dissonant. And the Controller is not ready yet...
- Sect of Alchemists has murdered the governor of the Iron Plains. They desire natural resources and secrets hidden beneath the Ea Charu.
- The construction of Hell Machine is supported by multiple groups. Excommunicated Virgil d'Armenty, crooked Alchemists, mad Salvatore Darne, and even the Fallen Children of Death. However they bicker and fight at every opportunity.
- Crassus Arrkoth is waiting for his opportunity, like a wolf outside the sheepfold. The defenses of the Hell Machine and the Sect of Alchemists are keeping him at bay.
- The Realm of Chthonians offered shelter to Virgil d'Armenty. When he allied with covert Alchemists, they feel betrayed and Sovereigns do not take betrayal kindly.
