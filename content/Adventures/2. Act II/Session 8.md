## Location descriptions
- **Korso** A small city, mostly known thanks to housing a printer of a well-known newspaper and its eccentric owner
- **Father Maxim's church** Large gothic cathedral, largely decrepit state. Inhabited by Maxim, Alexeyevich and Rufin
- **Emporium** large glass encased clock in the middle
- **Grotto** (grota), shrine of a White Witch, place of battle
- **Caerwyn** A large city build on lake canals full of buildings from bygone era. Stagnating but still beautiful. Large part of services is built on magic and automatons.
- **Darne Tower** Large mechanical clocktower that spans both above and below ground. Seems way larger from inside than from the outside.
- **Caerwyn University** Former centre of education, both the noble and foul kind. Unethical experiments caused the university to lose it's status. Most of the city is part of university complex, even though any educational processes are nowadays limited.
