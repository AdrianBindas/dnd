- Interested in this meteorite is also Independence Covenant and Kharak; Shadow Broker might send someone as well.
- The Order has been approached by an unknown figure who gave them intel how to cast the large dome.
- **Girth** and one agent possibly still alive. Possibly even **Theo** but difficult to judge given the circumstances.
- A certain farseeeing blind elf elder is a prominent figure in the independence movement.

- Thalassians are growing concerned as rumors of a long lost empire coming back from the dead.
- **Kane Keaton** embezzled large amount of money from the **Keaton** family and **Syndicate**. His motives are thought to be purely selfish but what is the truth?
