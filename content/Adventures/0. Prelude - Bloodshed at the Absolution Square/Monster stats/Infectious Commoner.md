___
>## Infectious Commoner
>*Medium Abberation, typically lawful evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
>- **Hit Points** 60 (+100 for brute)
>- **Speed** 20 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|'
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|18 (+4)|13 (+1)|19 (+4)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Int +6, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Insight +6, Intimidation +7, Perception +6
>- **Damage Resistances** poison
>- **Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned
>- **Senses** passive Perception 16
>- **Languages** Common
>- **Challenge** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +4
>___
>***Pack Tactics.*** The Infectious Commoner has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the incapacitated condition.  
>
>**Aberrant Form.** The Infectious Commoner exudes the chaos of the Far Realm. Any non-Aberration creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the zealot must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or take 7 (2d6) psychic damage.
>
>**Undead Fortitude.** If damage reduces the Infectious Commoner to 0 hit points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, the Infectious Commoner drops to 1 hit point instead.
>
>### Actions
>***Multiattack.*** The lieutenant makes two Cleave or Tentacle attacks. (three if brute)  
>
>***Cleave.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 20 (3d10 + 4) slashing damage. Creature hit must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. The creature contracts Withering Bloom disease on fail.
>
>**Tentacle.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* Target has the [grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_phb) condition (escape DC 16), on fail it is pulled next to the Infectious Commoner. While grappling the target, the Infectious Commoner can't make Tentacle attacks against other targets. When the Infectious Commoner moves, any Medium or smaller target it is grappling moves with it.
>
> ### Brute only actions
> 
> *Standing Leap*. The thri-kreen's long jump is up to 30 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.
> *Crushing Leap*. If the servant jumps at least 25 feet as part of its movement, it can then use this action to land on its feet in a space that contains one or more other creatures. Each of those creatures is pushed to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the servant and must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 13 (2d12) bludgeoning damage and is knocked [prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_phb). On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't knocked [prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_phb).
