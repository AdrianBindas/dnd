- woken up to a loud sound
- the village and surrounding forests are locked under a dome, looks like it was teleported, in fact it is just an illusion + force field
- a mysterious disease starts to spread, turns villagers into *something*
- under the dome are:
	- innkeep - could be a pleasant guy, one of the main NPCs
	- tavern guests - some prostitutes, gamblers, cleric of the Circle, some hidden cultists
	- elves - living in an enclave, all clues point to them
	- mercenaries - ruthless leader, Leonin with prosthesis snorting cocaine, one of the main NPCs
	- villagers - only a few, some of them will be cultists
		- church - the cleric there will be infected and choked or killed by an elven weapon
- the innkeep advocates taking care of the infected, mercenaries want to get rid of them
- if the mercenary leader gets angry, leaves and gets infected
- at the end the infected will storm the tavern (or other building they choose to defend)
- some elven ruins?
- some goblins under the village?
- a plot by the Circle to try out new population control, blame it on elves
- the barrier can come down any time, as other people are working on it, when it does, players get help from the outside. however it may also cause the disease to spread. at the end, players will have speech, fate of the Circle depends on what they say
