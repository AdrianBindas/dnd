## What is the artifact that was **Thiala** carrying?
#### Artifact
**Isolation Sphere**
Metalic contraption consisting of multiple rings of different sizes, each rotating around a core which radiates with dim light. This item was developed in collaboration between Verdugo Syndicate and University of Ellyria. 

You can perform a **10-minute ritual** to activate it. When activated, the sphere expands an invisible field in a **15 -foot radius** (or 5 meters) centered on itself. All **nonliving matter** within that radius is drawn inward and suspended in perfect temporal stasis, contained within the core.
Items placed within remain in their exact condition until released. The sphere can store only one “containment field” at a time, if activated again while full, the previous contents are irrevocably lost to the void.
If a **creature** is caught in the activation radius, it must succeed on a **DC 20 Constitution saving throw** or be instantly **disintegrated**, leaving behind only dust. On a success, the creature is instead **pushed 10 feet away** from the sphere’s center as the field collapses around them.

To release the contents, the wielder must perform another **10-minute ritual**. When completed, the stored materials manifest in a space of its exact original configuration

Each use of the Isolation Sphere leaves a faint arcane residue detectable by *detect magic* spell. The artifact was never fully perfected, each use carrying a risk of causing an anomaly. After every activation, roll a d20; on a 1, a dimensional anomaly appears within 30 feet.
