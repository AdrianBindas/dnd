Svet v Útrobách je úplne odlišný od sveta vonku. V stiesnených priestoroch medzi tkanivom sa premávajú bytosti poznačené týmto svetom, ktoré sa v Útrobách narodili a ktoré tu svoju púť aj skončia, bez toho aby uvideli svetlo materiálneho sveta. Tieto chodby ale lákajú aj mnohých, ktorí sem zablúdili z dôvodov sakrálnych, akademických alebo rýdzo sebeckých.
Najväčšou osadou je Gildore, pozostávajúca z niekoľkých tisíc obyvateľov, ktorej mramorové veže existovali v Útrobách dávno pred tým, než si ktokoľvek pamätá. Obyvateľstvo pozostáva z kňazov a kňažiek Pani, šamanov uctievajúcich prírodných spiritov, fanatikov, akademikov a lovcov pokladov. Z mramorovej katedrály dohliada na Gildore Pramatka, hlavná kňažná. Bioluminiscenčné lampy Gildoru osvetľujú okolité tunely a poskytujú bezpečie v tomto nehostinnom mieste.
Ale Gildore je v porovnaní s Útrobami iba zrnkom piesku. V chodbách, kde svetlo katedrály nedosiahne číhajú nie len nebezpečné tvory, mäsožravé rastliny ale aj zmutovaní pútnici, ktorým už nie je pomoci, fanatici odmietajúci láskavosť Pani a humanoidné bytosti, ktoré už dávno zavrhli svoju ľudskosť. Cestovatelia môžu naraziť na roztratené tábory a malé usadlosti ľudí, ktorí odmietli prístrešie Gidoru.
Aspoň takto vyzerali Útroby pred tým, než sa pred rokmi zjavil Uriel Cabeth. Priniesol so sebou tú chorobu, rakovinu, ktorej hovorí Rozkvet a šíril zvesti o Morovom kráľovi. Netrvalo dlho a Gildore podľahol. Niektorí sa nechali presvedčiť, niektorí stratili odvahu a Rozkvet ich ovládol všetkých. Aby sa zabránilo rozšíreniu choroby, kňazi sa stiahli z Gildoru a vytvorili Membránu, ktorá chráni zvyšok Útrob pred Urielovým vplyvom. Je ale iba otázkou času, kým Membrána padne a tá pliaga nás pohltí. Osada kňazov, ktorí sa starajú o membránu nemá dostatok zásob pre všetkých a teda tí, ktorí prežili, sa rozpŕchli po celých Útrobách. Niektorí sa snažia nájsť cestu von, iní hľadajú liek. Ale väčšina sa stala súčasťou temných Útrob.

Vol 1. The Camp

The Bone whistle Piotr brought proved useful, may he find peace. The four-winged creatures left me alone for a short while.

I reached the Shallows. The cancer does not spread this far. Yet. I don't know what to expect, not many of us have been this far from the New Gilmore.

The Rot is next. I've heard the stories, and now I've seen the edge of it from here: a stench of decay so thick you'll be sick for hours without a cloth over your mouth. The ground moves beneath the corpses. Something enormous rolls underneath, I don't want to think about it. I just have to find a way out.

I don't know what I expected. The mass graves, the lands covered by our people and then just. Nothing. As if the world just ended, as if someone cut right through it. I wanted a way out, but this is madness, just a spit in my face, after everything I sacrificed.

Maybe not all is lost. Someone lives here, just the edge of the cliff. Shadowy figure with feathers, kenku maybe. While I do not trust them, they might be my only hope. 

If I don't find a help I need, I will find a way out myself. You know, the stars. The stars are calling me.

Oh Katya, Lyren, I am so sorry.


Vol 2. The Mouth

We have to climb the Mouth. A vertical hole in Her flesh. Magic dies here, torches sputter, compasses spin, holy symbols go cold. Only the small alcoves carved into the walls offer refuge.

These vines are not what they seem. They pulse. I touched one; my hand blistered for two days. Are they veins?

I reached the Ossuary. The stories were real - Lady's effigy weeps real tears, warm to the touch. The petrified remains around her spoke to me. I wish others were here to listed to Her words.

Piotr is gone. He went willingly, smiling. Said the Plague King showed him a vision of his dead wife. I watched him climb into the fallen tower on the other side. I did not have strength to stop him, I feel weak after the long climb. Very weak. I heard voices from the tower, he was not alone in there. I'm alone now. Kaz dead. Sera gone. Piotr lost.

I'll keep going west. To the Camp, then the Rot, then the Edge of the World. For Katya.


Vol 3. Bile Lake

Sera left us in the Carnivorous Grove. She went to hunt. Walked into the ferns. We heard her shout once. Then nothing. I tried looking for her but… The grove sends shivers down my spine. The ferns snap shut on anything that moves. Hidden sinkholes everywhere. And the deer, oh gods.

Now we're at Bile Lake. The waterfall from the Ossuary drops into it. The yellow-green liquid burns skin on contact — it ate through Kaz’s armor in seconds. He didn't scream long.

There's an obelisk on the lake floor, glowing faintly. Don't touch it. Piotr swam close and started muttering about the Plague King. His eyes went distant.
