## Front 1: The Scholar
**Description**: A deceptive and petty god of scholars and knowledge, the Seer.
**Motivation**: Archiving history gets boring. Eventually you'll want to be someone who makes history.
**Goals and subfronts**:
- Cause a conflict between Malady and the living world.
- Cause a war between Kharak and Arrkoth.
**Timeline**:
- Ressurected Uriel Cabeth and guided him to spread the will of Malady.
- Provided Imperial Church with information for their experiements.
## Front 2: Azathoth
**Description**: Worm of the Worlds, child of the Void and a revolting creature with neverending hunger.
**Motivation**: The planets and their inhabitants are quite tasty. However he lacks to means to overwhelm such civilizations and relies on the help of more powerful beings - like Malady.
**Goals and subfronts**:
- Got into the mind of Zaine, wants to manipulate him.
- Some elves who imprisoned him see the Azathoth as a tool which could help them with the human scum. Now they poisoned the Heart of the Wilds to weaken their opposition.
- An accursed family near Grimwald is preparing for his return.
**Timeline**: 
- When he attacked the world, he got imprisoned by the ancient elf civilization, the mirrors were meant to show him the world he cannot devour. 
- One halfling who broke the Grimwald mirror got cannibalized and the shards spread and created a tight-knit family of Azathoth's believers.
