This story unfolds around 700 years before the Arrkoth Empire was founded and invaded Orynthia

Our name has risen from simple hunters to stewards and protectors. What began as humble wardens of forest and fields grew into lords of this mountain and vale, sworn to guard these lands from harm. For generations, House de Veneris stood among the oldest and most esteemed bloodlines of the realm, our halls alive with music. Our granaries overflowed with a golden wheat and our cellers were filled to the brim with the finest wines. Back then, our name was spoken with a reverence that bordered on myth. 
Yet heavens have not been kind. Seasons turned against us and misfortune crept in where prosperity once reigned. The last few years have brought blight to the fields, fire to the forests, and debts to our gates. Harvests withered before they could be gathered, the deer fled deeper into woods, and the rivers that once ran clear now carry sickness in their waters.
Death, too, has walked among us. One by one, our kin, our servants and our allies disappeared into the nothingness. And who survived, fled as far as they could, spreading rumors like wildfire.
The home of our family is nearly in ruins, the rains weep through the halls of our once opulent, now desolate and accursed mansion. It is as though the house itself mourns with us, walls burdened by grief and seeping through with sorrow.

Phoebe - A village herbalist and medic, cheerful and kind young woman living on the outskirts of the village. Common folk think she is a bit too eccentric and strange and avoid her. Nonetheless, she tends to them all the same, whether it is a broken bone or a common cold. Your good friend and lover.

Eadwund de Veneris - Head of de Veneris family, one of its last two surviving members, your father. Old and sickly man. Misfortune after misfortune has befallen the family during his lifetime. Though he did what he could to preserve its legacy, the family possessions turned to ash and dust. After Vanessa, his wife and your mother, suddenly died, he turned bitter, angry and cold.

Matthias - An old man living at the end of the village, known for distilling whiskey in his shed. Sober only on rare occasions. Your friend and drinking companion. Childless and alove, he took care of Ophelia when she was little and has watched over her ever since.

PC - This is you, heir of de Veneris family, one of its last two surviving members. You are a hunter by trade, sworn to protect the village from wolves, bears and worse things that would dare to endanger its people. Young and reckless, sneaking away every night. One day you will bear the family's name and its misfortune, but for now, youth is yours to spend.
