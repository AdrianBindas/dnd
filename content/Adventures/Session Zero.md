##### Scheduling
- [ ] *Start date*
	- [ ] When do we want to start?
- [ ] *Session length and format*
	- [ ] How long should one session last?
	- [ ] How should the sessions be played? Online or offline?
	- [ ] Retrospective minutes each month or two
- [ ] *Session cancellation conditions*
	- [ ] Would you like to play even though one player is missing?
##### Gameplay content
- [ ] *Themes and genre
	- [ ] What genre would you enjoy the most? Exploration, investigation, horror, action, politics...
	- [ ] Do you enjoy more a heroic fantasy or a gritty fantasy?
	- [ ] Do you enjoy humor as a part of the story and roleplay?
- [ ] *Expectations of each player*
	- [ ] Why are you enjoying playing DnD? Is it combat, roleplaying, story crafting, exploring, mystery solving...
	- [ ] What are you hoping to experience during the adventures? Fun with friends or something more?
- [ ] *Character death*
	- [ ] Is it fine if your character dies permanently? With or without your consent?
- [ ] *Out of bounds subjects*
	- [ ] Politics/religion
	- [ ] Racism/sexism/misogyny
	- [ ] Sex/rape
	- [ ] Torture/mutilation/gore
- [ ] *Player Agency*
	- [ ] Is GM or other player allowed to roleplay your character?
	- [ ] Is player allowed to create a deity/location/NPC as a part of their backstory? 
##### Balancing and rules
- [ ] *Balance*
	- [ ] Currently unbalanced
	- [ ] Plan is to employ iterative balancing during the adventure
	- [ ] Is unbalanced game still fun? How is GM allowed to nerf an OP character?
- [ ] *Rules*
	- [ ] Introduction of the homebrewed rules
	- [ ] Do we want to implement rationing?
	- [ ] What about Short rest/long rest time span changes?
- [ ] *Rule of the Cool*
	- [ ] How strict should the ruling be?
- [ ] *Homebrew*
	- [ ] Can we use and create homebrew content?
- [ ] *Inspiration*
	- [ ] Is inspiration mechanics interesting to you?
	- [ ] How should the inspiration be gained?
	- [ ] How should the inspiration be used?
##### Upcoming adventures
- [ ] *World*
	- [ ] Short introduction of the homebrewed world
- [ ] *Species*
	- [ ] Species have currently missing images, any ideas?
	- [ ] Q/A
- [ ] *One-shot*
	- [ ] Q/A
- [ ] *Campaign*
	- [ ] Simple pitch
	- [ ] How long should the campaign last?
	- [ ] At what level do the characters start?
- [ ] *One on one campaigns*
	- [ ] Is anyone interested in One on one campaign?
	- [ ] Alternatively, smaller (2-3 man) campaigns?
