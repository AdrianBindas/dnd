Information on the traitor Kane Keaton:
- Internal conflicts between members of the Syndicate provided an opportunity for a certain Kane Keaton to embezzle a large amount of money from the Syndicate. Valuables in a form of gold, jewelry and gemstones were drained from multiple vaults and banks and exported out of the city via unknown means.
  Kane Keaton was previously deemed loyal and was working for Syndicate for 20 years, many times closely with I. F. Verdugo. He is a brother of Alphonse Keaton, leader of a moderate revolutionary wing, but investigators cleared Alphonse of any suspicions. However it is believed Kane did not act alone, probable ties include Kharak Dominion and Lord Dunhart of the Eternal Court.
  Kane is currently on the loose, leaving the city along with all the goods. Current objectives are securing valuables, locating and eliminating the traitor Kane Keaton.
Information on Markuss of the Eternal Court:

Information on Veyric De Venaris:
- House De Venaris is an ancient and respected one who, during old times, build their legacy as a dynasty of hunters. Changing world eventually forced them to find a new way, way of doctors and alchemists, using the knowledge gained from hunting to create drugs, poisons and concoctions. Oriven De Venaris was a proponent of the old ways, a skilled hunter, even the Syndicate hired him multiple times when things got too tough, while his mother was a mistress of alchemy and medicine, not too much is known about her. When both died under suspicious circumstances, head of the family became Nivara De Venaris, Veyric's only sister. Two siblings soon went their own ways, Veyric became an anonymous travelling hunter, while Nivara extended the influence and power of house De Venaris further than ever before.
