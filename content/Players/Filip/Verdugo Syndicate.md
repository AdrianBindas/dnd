**File on Ismael Francisco Verdugo:**
First time someone would meet Ismael F. Verdugo, they could confuse him with just another quiet fellow in an off-the-shelf suit - someone they’d pass on the street without remembering. Short, a little on the skinny side, hair always cut too practically to be fashionable. But the thing about Verdugo is, the moment he opens his mouth, the room changes. Never wasting words, but when he speaks, people stop thinking about what they were going to say and start wondering how to follow what he just said. 

He didn’t just inherit his father’s money; he multiplied it dozen times. He’s got a nose for deals the way other men have a taste for wine. He thrives on risk; saying, the moment you feel comfortable is the moment you’re already falling behind. 

Few know much about his private life. Verdugo keeps it behind a wall and even fewer dare to ask. But the whispers are hard to ignore - he’s got a wife, but also children here and there, each with a different mother. Never flaunted, never explained. Definitely not a man you’d call warm, but he rewards loyalty. Very generously. And that act is nothing more than just a calculated part of his success.


**File on Verdugo Syndicate:**
Many don't understand the Verdugo Syndicate. It’s not a just a company or few - it’s a system. A web of factories, foundries, shipping firms, insurance houses, banks, mines, and mills, all linked by contracts and cross-ownership so tight that you can’t pull one thread without tugging on the rest. That’s the Verdugo Syndicate whose touch reaches every part of our modern life.

Most employees will live and die believing the Syndicate is nothing more than a titan of industry and finance. But buried in the immense machinery are other functions. Quiet departments with names that don’t appear on the public organizational charts. 

- **Retrievals & Acquisition (R&A)** works under the heading of “Special Asset Recovery Office”. Officially, they chase unpaid debts and secure collateral. Unofficially, they dispatch operatives to seize certain objects, sometimes from half a world away. The R&A is internally divided into multiple specialized divisions:
	- **Commerce and logistics division** facilitates purchase of high-value goods, handles import of special commodities, arranges quiet acquisitions of artifacts, coordinates transport of goods and secures handover points
	- **External relations division** manages Syndicate representation to foreign organizations and guilds, negotiates directly with artifact owners (bribes, political pressure, threats)
	- **Survey division** identifies archeological sites, abandoned fortresses and ruins, marks safe routes for recovery teams and flags obstacles (political and environmental)
	- **Recovery division** fields operatives tasked with physical acquisition of artifacts using stealth, purchase or force

- **Identification office** falls within the “Scholarly valuation office”. To the untrained eye, they’re just assessors of antique furniture, rare manuscripts, and colonial artifacts. In reality, they’re thaumaturges, cryptologists and enchanters, capable of detecting a dangerous magic beneath a coat of platina.

- **Arcane Containment Bureau (ACB)** hides in the rolls of the Syndicate’s security contracts. While most of their guards patrol warehouses and counting houses, a select cadre stands watch over vaults that no insurance policy could ever cover.

All of it amounts to one thing: while ninety-nine parts of the Syndicate move the world through commerce, finance and industry, one quiet, well-funded part is assembling a collection of magical artifacts, ages old goods and odd curiosities. What they intend to do with it is above anyone's status to know.
