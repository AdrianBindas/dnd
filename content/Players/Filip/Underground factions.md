- Imperial Investigation Bureau
	- Out there are some who decide to relinquish everything and more to become executors of the Empress' will. Some do so out of their devotion and faith to the throne, others are chasing respect or thrill, while the rest is persuaded by more basic incentives. Nevertheless, it is said no one who was offered a position in the Bureau has ever refused. What happens after they join is ultimately a mystery.
	  Most information suggest that their number is probably quite low, so the members tend to work alone or in small squads. Members of the Bureau are deployed on a wide range of tasks including sabotages, intelligence gathering, assassinations but also crucial diplomatic talks, assessments of the enemy territory or population and internal security. Their existence sparked many spine-chilling stories amongst the population of Orynthia, but the truth will probably never be known.
- Verdugo Syndicate
    - Led by a successful businessman and entrepreneur Ismael Francisco Verdugo, the Collective for the Recovery and Acquisition of Valuable Exhibits is an international organization with the goal of amassing a collection of antiques of various nature. These antiques are displayed as a part of an exhibition or a museum for the general public or curious experts. At least that's the official story.
      Under the surface, the organization is a part of the Verdugo Syndicate, a consortium of multiple wealthy organizations utilizing their immense power and wealth to acquire objects of their interest. While the Syndicate is composed of multiple organizations and companies operating in different fields, such as banking or manufactory, their primary focus is a collection and trade of  invaluable relics. They often set their sights on magical artifacts, technological marvels, scientific inventions, ancient relics, or art pieces such as paintings and statues. These are at first closely inspected and registered, and the truly interesting and valuable pieces are hidden in protected vaults while the more common objects are sold on a black market. One particularly renowned illegal market, run largely by the Verdugo Syndicate is called the Dusk Exchange. Located in the Ellyrian undercity, it offers illegal goods, magical items, art pieces and exotic relics.
      Whether the motive behind the tireless hunt for the precious antiques is pure monetary gain or the reasons are deeper and more sinister is unknown. But one thing is sure - the Verdugo Syndicate amassed the largest collection of magical items, relics and invaluable artifacts across the known world and it's not stopping anytime soon.
- Children of Maer
	- The Imperium has countless enemies. Amidst the most vehement is a non-violent group who call themselves Children of Maer. The group is highly illegal and considered a terrorist organization by the Imperial government, as their activities include a wide range sabotage of Imperial systems and departments, political activism, espionage, and other covert tactics utilizing arcane arts and technology alike. Due to the ongoing relentless hunt on the Children of Maer by the government, members tend to appear only in their signature outfit incorporating the sigil of the faction and a mask concealing the identity of the wearer.
	  The Children of Maer advocate for the personal freedom of each individual, opposing repression and limitations imposed by the government or outright rejecting the existence and authority of any government. This staunch view causes difficulties gaining new supporters or alliances. However their tactics and devotion are not to be taken lightly.
- Shadow Broker
	- Whether you are a high ranking officer, a rising business owner or a lowly adventurer, you can be sure of one thing - The Shadow Broker *knows*. They employ not only a network of moles but also complex mechanical wonders and dark arts to gather knowledge and dirt on everyone in Ellyria. The information is afterwards sold to the highest bidder, while the potential buyers are chosen by the Shadow Broker themself. 
	  Not much is known of this elusive shadow. Some say the Shadow Broker is a group, some believe it's a single individual or a title, that can be claimed or inherited. In fact, once in a while a pretender tends to appear, claiming to be *the* Broker; of course, their lifespan is halted short soon thereafter. Others swear the Shadow Broker is a deity, a spirit or even a goddess of secrets, Morgena, herself. In the end no one can be sure and the Shadow Broker remains a myth, a legend, amidst the citizens of Ellyria. 
- The Eternal Court
	- The parties, gatherings and balls of the Eternal Court are said to be more lavish and extravagant than those of the royal family. Full of the highest quality wines, exotic food and beautiful woman, these flamboyant events last for days. But under the guise of the grandeur and splendor, lies a jungle of intrigue, politics and money. Composed of the largest and most influential criminal elites of Ellyria, the Eternal Court controls the network of traffickers, casinos, drug dealers, thieves and brothels spread throughout the city. While the internal affairs of the Court are a messy and often brutal game, when the Court is united, its influence can be felt far beyond the walls of Ellyria.
	  Nowadays, the oppression by the Imperial Church has united the Eternal Court more than ever before and it is known to be a staunch and powerful supporter of the revolution against the Arrkoth Imperium. In addition, the Court is a sponsor of the Inverted University, an enigmatic and outlawed academy of arcane arts too sinister and too morbid to be approved.
