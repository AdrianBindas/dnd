## Foreword
Ever since aeons long past, the house of de Venaris is on the hunt for the most foul, most wicked creatures this world has ever seen. Mere presence of those beasts or aberrations, as we call them, sow terror and madness to the hearts of many, while their sharp claws, fangs and appendages tear apart the innocent. Giving rise to malice and violence, preying out of indulgence not necessity, those daemoniacal things may not, *shall not* continue the onslaught and the house of de Venaris will make sure of that. It is our duty.
## Classification
What follows is a comprehensive classification by Roderic de Venaris based on the ancient texts by Hardbein Skardisson of Vinterhavn.
1. Primal aberrations
2. Higher aberrations
3. Divergent aberrations
4. Chthonians
5. Infected Ones
---
### 1. Primal aberrations
Represent the most rudimentary class of aberrant entities. They exhibit minimal cognitive ability, relying instead on instinctual drives for predation and territorial defense. Morphologically, they tend toward robust, often armored forms, optimized for brute force rather than subtlety.
#### Hunger Drudge
*Description:* 1-2 meters long crawling 'carcass' with dozens of needle-sharp teethed orifices of various sizes. Fast and agile movement using multiple pairs of insect-like legs. 
*Environment:* Forests, dungeons and rural communities. 
*Behavior:* Driven by hunger and vibrations, aggressively and mindlessly hunting anything that moves. Typically found in groups.
*Strengths:* Wounds caused by a Drudge tend to be poisoned or get infected.
*Weaknesses:* Blind but sensitive to vibrations. Fire and cold deters it, emits loud shrieking noise.
#### Gutcoil
*Description:* A mass of oily, brown coils resembling intestines or wet cords, endlessly twisting around a calcified nucleus. Lacks face, limbs, or eyes. Its movements produce a sucking, wet drag against stone and bone alike.
*Environment:* Places of violence.
*Behavior:* Necrophage, opportunist, individualist. Can constrict with sudden, unnatural speed. Victims are often found with joints shattered inward, torsos compressed.
*Strengths:* Able to quickly constrict multiple individuals, make sure to travel in groups.
*Weaknesses:* Nucleus, though well-protected, brittle when exposed to sudden cold.
#### Crowned Manticore
*Description:* A massive dark-purple hexapod encased in pale bone plates. At a distance resembles a large lion or panther; however joints bend the wrong way, the membrane skin overgrown with short spikes. Head is a split, vertical mouth rimmed with teeth facing backward made to tear flesh of its victims. Six eyes able to see in a total darkness. The long muscular tail ends in a knotted sphere of poisoned stingers.
*Environment:* Dense forests, tropical jungles.
*Behavior:* Some specimen able to imitate animal sounds drawing in prey. Highly territorial. Known to stalk prey.
*Strengths:* Heavily armored. Tail used to pull victims closer and poison them. Perfect eyesight, able to detect even invisible prey.
*Weaknesses:* Dislikes and avoids fire and direct sunlight.
### 2. Higher aberrations
Entities possessing advanced problem-solving faculties and display deliberate, goal-oriented behavior. They frequently engage in strategic planning, ambush tactics, and manipulation of lesser intelligences. Their societies, when present, exhibit hierarchical structures.
#### Polyform
*Description:* Columnar organism with radial symmetry; height varies between 2 to 4 meters. Upper segments display five flaps used as sensory organs. 5 bat-like wings grow out of the middle segments. Movement using dense, retractable protrusions at the base or using wings.
*Environment:* Southern frozen parts of Orynthia
*Behavior:* No clear indication of emotion or intent. Engages in complex construction and social behavior with other specimen of it's kind. Seems to study environments through systematic disassembly of local matter. Has exhibited neither aggression nor mercy—only dissection. Known to communicate using induced vibrations in stone or liquid
*Strengths:* Intelligent behavior, mastery of arcane.
*Weaknesses:* Unknown.
#### Architect
*Description:* A towering, faceless entity of stretched tissue and bone structures masked as a physical structure such as a house or other building. Constantly reshapes its body.
*Environment:* Urban.
*Behavior:* Traps victims inside its shifting flesh-labyrinth. Victims report hearing familiar voices calling from deeper inside. Few emerge.
*Strengths:* Alters perception of space; its interiors do not obey natural laws of geometry or time. Outer structures made out of bone-like material, hard to damage.
*Weaknesses:* Tracking one's path through the flesh-labyrinth helps with navigation, this method is however not completely reliable. Starving it out and forcing the specimen to actively hunt reveals soft vulnerable parts.
#### Lurker of the Deep
*Description:* A vast intelligence inhabiting subterranean aquifers and fathomless caverns. It appears as an amorphous shifting mass of a bioluminescent ichor with countless eyes.
*Environment:* Resides in the deep parts of the Underworld across Orynthia and Vesper.
*Behavior:* Tendency to observe the prey and manipulate its mind and lurk it into the deeper parts of the caverns.
*Strengths:* Accesses and manipulates memories of individuals who get close by. Communicates through visions, dreams and hallucinations. Able to influence subterranean terrain, causing fissures or collapses. Difficult to approach due to high perception and ability to retreat one of many cracks or fissures.
*Weaknesses:* Susceptible to arcane. Recommended approach is maximizing the first strike as longer fights favor the Lurker.
### 3. Divergents
Anthropoid beings whose form and behavior resemble those of sentient races. Despite their superficially benign or cooperative presentation, documented cases indicate an underlying predisposition for deceit and falseness. Social engineering has tendency to precede predatory behavior.
#### False Bride
*Description:* Humanoid creature able to change form and take on various appearances. Many times wearing the face of a loved one, relative or a  lost friend. Close inspection reveals imperfections such as eyes that do not blink in unison or breath that carries no heat.
*Environment:* Urban.
*Behavior:* Contrary to succubus or incubus, a Bride does not seduce, it is drawn to longing and loneliness. It seeks out the lonely, the obsessed, the lost and left behind. Taking a form of any humanoid the Bride might cause real-like visions or dreams. Over time the Bride manifests physically. Feeding on the attention and emotions, the creature causes the victim to slowly deteriorate. First, erasing the memories and damaging sanity. Then withering away the body of a victim until they become a vessel of obsession, repeating phrases or gestures until they rot away with a smile on their face.
*Strengths:* Difficult to identify, similar to veilkin.
*Weaknesses:* Low combat capabilities. Can be disrupted by helping the victim.
#### Night Stalker
*Description:* Appears as a towering, gaunt humanoid, with grotesquely elongated limbs and a hunching spine. The face is featureless except for a faint impression of eye sockets and a subtle bulge where a mouth should be. Arms and legs can extend farther than their length suggests, and the entity often moves silently across vertical surfaces or ceilings.
*Environment:* Dark and shadowy places.
*Behavior:* Once Night Stalker marks a victim, they will begin to notice it in their peripheral view, such as on rooftops or in dark alleyways. The Stalker gets closer each time and appears more frequently. Eventually victim goes mad, falls into vegetative states or plainly disappears.
*Strengths:* Able to traverse and hide in any environment. Long reach thanks to it's elongated arms.
*Weaknesses:* Takes a long time before finally attacking it's prey. Sometimes gives up the chase and chooses another victim.
### 4. Antediluvians
Primeval entities of immense power, believed to have emerged before the humanoid races that shaped the world. However their true origins and motives are essentially unknown. They exert profound influence using dreams, arcane, and even the flow of time itself. 
#### V'aathra
*Description:* Motionless sphere of tissue, ulcers, membranes, tentacles and countless other gruesome appendages with a diameter of a few dozens of meters. Suspended from the bottom on a thick appendage is a tumor assumed to be its head, with wide mouth devoid of any teeth.
*Environment:* Unknown, communicates only through dreams and visions. All attempts to find its location had failed.
*Behavior:* Manipulates victims to cause slaughter and chaos. Believed to be a source of aberrant infestation in Valdengard, 23rd year of the Empire. Infestation was dealt with by destroying a local cult worshipping V'aathra.
*Strengths:* Unknown.
*Weaknesses:* Unknown.
#### Tsathoggua
*Description:* Its head is of a monstrous toad with sleepy lids half-lowered over its globular eyes. The head sits on top of large obese belly covered in a mix of green slimy skin and a mockery of a short fur. Its four sloth-like appendages are too meager and too disfigured to support its weight. Thathoggua is a being as powerful as it is hideous. 
*Environment:* Dark and shadowy places under Orynthia.
*Behavior:* A deity or a spirit found most of the time to be in deep slumber. If disturbed, he will devour whoever awakened him, unless the awakener has a sacrifice to offer instead. Too slothful to meddle in the affairs of mortals.
*Strengths:* Unknown. Its strength is thought to be comparable to a minor god. Avoid direct confrontation. More research needed.
*Weaknesses:* Unknown.
### 5. Infected Ones
They represent previously sapient organisms altered through direct contact with aberrant vectors (e.g., bodily fluids or arcane transmissions). They exhibit hybridized physiology: retention of humanoid structure alongside emergent aberrant traits. Behavioral studies document progressive cognitive deterioration. For a long time regarded as legends and myths as no confirmed cases were recoded. First confirmed and examined encounter occurred until Bloodshed at the Absolution Square in Ellyria, 340th year of the Empire.
