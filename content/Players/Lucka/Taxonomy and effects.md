## **Beasts**
**Nature:** Mundane or quasi-magical animals, shaped by the natural world.  
**Subtypes:**
- **Mammals:** Wolves, bears, lions, bats, giant elk
- **Avians:** Eagles, owls, griffons, giant vultures
- **Reptilians:** Snakes, crocodiles, dinosaurs
- **Aquatic:** Sharks, octopi, whales
- **Insects:** Giant spiders, giant scorpions, giant wasps
- **Amphibians:** Giant frogs, toads, axebeaks (semi-aquatic)  

## **Dragons and pseudodragons**
**Nature:** Magical, highly intelligent reptilian beings tied to elemental power.  
- **Chromatic:** Red, blue, green, black, white (evil-aligned)
- **Metallic:** Gold, silver, brass, bronze, copper (good-aligned)
- **Gem:** Amethyst, sapphire, emerald, topaz, crystal (psionic/neutral)
- **Lesser Draconic Kin:** Wyverns, pseudodragons, dragon turtles, guard drakes

## **Aberrations**
**Nature:** Beings warped by alien influence or extraplanar reality.  
**Notes:** Consult with Veyric

## **Undead**
**Nature:** Once-living beings animated by necromantic force.  
- **Mindless Undead:** Skeletons, zombies, crawling claws
- **Cursed Souls:** Wraiths, specters, banshees, ghosts
- **Self-Aware Undead:** Vampires, liches, death knights
- **Necrotic Constructs:** Mummies, bone nagas, dracoliches  

## **Humanoids**
**Nature:** Sapient bipeds with societal structure and culture.  
- **Common Races:** Humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, gnomes
- **Monstrous Races:** Orcs, goblins, kobolds, gnolls, lizardfolk
- **Hybrid Races:** Yuan-ti, minotaurs, centaurs, tabaxi

## **Monstrosities**
**Nature:** Creatures that defy normal biological or magical classification.  
- **Hybrid Beasts:** Owlbears, chimeras, manticores
- **Arcane Experiments:** Basilisks, bulettes, displacer beasts
- **Predatory Abominations:** Hydra, kraken spawn, ropers

## **Plants**
**Nature:** Sentient or mobile vegetation, often magically awakened.  
- **Sapient Flora:** Treants, awakened trees, shambling mounds
- **Fungal Colonies:** Myconids, violet fungi, gas spores
- **Predatory Flora:** Assassin vines, corpse flowers, twig blights
