# Lore consistency review

This is a continuity audit, not a canon rewrite. Existing notes and adventure material were left intact; the items below identify decisions to make before future public edits.

## General canon picture

The setting's stable through-line is a technologically advanced early-modern world where arcana, industry, imperial politics, and the Labyrinth overlap. Orynthia is dominated by Arrkoth while Vesper contains major rivals such as Kharak, and the active campaign moves from Syndicate and Redguard conflicts into questions about the Blind Messengers, Malady, Azathoth, the House of Death, and the returning powers of the Labyrinth.

## Major continuity issues

### High priority: de Venaris / de Veneris
The active session notes, player material, and several DM notes use **de Venaris**, while campaign planning, Nivara references, and *Our Venerable Deed* frequently use **de Veneris**. Decide whether one is the canonical family spelling and treat the other as an alias, historical transliteration, or typo; the player-facing NPC registry currently follows the more common active-session form, **de Venaris**.

### High priority: the origin of Veyric's beast
The family arc has at least three incompatible explanations: Thagruk and Girthak bestowed a dangerous gift, the de Veneris line inherited a taint connected to Spawn of Malady, and the beast is a prototype of an ideal kenku created by kenku makers. These can work as competing in-world theories, but the DM notes should eventually label which is true, which is propaganda, and which is incomplete.

### High priority: Azathoth's status and relationship to the campaign
*The Story So Far* says Azathoth died thousands of years ago, while later sessions and planning describe Azathoth as imprisoned, conscious through mirrors, and seeking freedom, a body, or revenge against elves. The notes also vary between Azathoth as an independent planet-devouring being, a patron connected to Zaine, and a power working alongside or beneath Malady; establish whether “dead” means physically dead, dormant, imprisoned, or merely believed dead.

### High priority: Arrkoth chronology
The Orynthia timeline places Crassus Arrkoth's conquest about 340 years ago, while the Arrkoth timeline describes an empire founded in Vesper before fleeing to Orynthia and beginning its conquest in Year 3 of the imperial reign. *Our Venerable Deed* says its story occurs 700 years before the Empire was founded, so the setting needs one calendar and a clear distinction between the older Arrkoth bloodline, the Vesperian empire, and the later Orynthian conquest.

### High priority: the meteorite's chain of custody
Early Act I notes say the Order discovered the meteorite and used it in the Withering Bloom, later notes say the Imperial Church acquired it, and the Redguard arc says the Duke will show it to the party at Loth Rakk. The later Loth Rakk material places the object and Subject 0 in the ruins, but the transport between the Order, Church, Redguard, and Loth Rakk is not stated consistently; add a short dated chain of custody.

### Medium priority: Ehaageris, Voreth, and Voormithadretha
The public world names Ehaageris as the Hanged Man and god of death, while DM and Redguard notes use Voreth, Vorath, and Voormithadretha for a previous or ancient god of death and sometimes describe the Duke and Onos as fragments of that being. Decide whether these are alternate names for one entity, successive gods, or fragments with distinct identities, and standardize the spelling of each surviving name.

### Medium priority: the disappearance of the Hanged Man
The Act II outline says Ehaageris disappeared three years ago, while an Act I note describes him as missing for only several months. This may be an in-world report made at different campaign dates, but the campaign calendar should identify the current date and the canonical duration.

### Medium priority: Nivara's status and ritual
The campaign recap calls Nivara murdered, while the later investigation correctly treats that event as an apparent murder that becomes an abduction; later notes rescue her and describe a pregnancy and a planned sacrifice. The intended sacrifice also varies between Nivara and her child, Nivara and her unborn daughter, Phoebe, and Veyric as a possible substitute, so the ritual's required participants and current plan need one authoritative version.

### Medium priority: Uriel Cabeth's name and origin
The dominant spelling is **Uriel Cabeth**, but **Cabath** appears in the campaign recap and session notes. His origin also varies between a half-orc/half-elf adopted by halflings who found Ammenmesse, a resurrected agent guided by the Seer, and a servant or preacher of Malady; these may be stages of one history, but the sequence and patron should be fixed.

### Medium priority: Sussane's identity and spelling
The Redguard material alternates between **Sussane**, **Susanne**, **Sophia**, and **Jess** for the child who travels with Onyx. Confirm whether these are aliases, separate characters, or draft names, then use one public name and reserve any aliases for deliberate reveals.

### Low priority: Ansben's fate wording
The sessions describe Ansben as disappearing or sinking underground, while the recap describes the villagers as slaughtered and fused into a spiral. This can be reconciled as rumor versus the party's later observation, but the published recap should use one phrasing and note that the initial reports were misleading.

### Low priority: names and titles in translation
The same figures and places appear under English, Slovak, and transliterated forms: Duke/Vojvoda, Hanged Man/Obesenec, Redguard/Vojvodstvo, and Aldric Headstrong/Aldric Tvrdá Hlava. Keeping translations in dialogue is useful, but titles and proper names should have one searchable canonical form in headings and links.

## Recommended cleanup order

1. Choose canonical spellings for the de Venaris family, Uriel Cabeth, Sussane, Voreth, and Voormithadretha.
2. Create a single dated campaign timeline covering the Absolution Square incident, Verdugo's assassination attempt, Redguard, Loth Rakk, Al'Zafira, and the current Korso/House of Death arc.
3. Write one secret DM-only truth each for Azathoth, the de Venaris beast, the meteorite, Ehaageris's disappearance, and Nivara's ritual; preserve conflicting public accounts as deliberate misinformation where useful.
4. After those decisions, update existing files in place rather than deleting or replacing them, and keep the three World registries as the player-facing navigation layer.
