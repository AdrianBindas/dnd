# Common symptoms
| d12 | Symptom                 | Description                                       |
| --- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Persistent cough        | Dry or wet; worsens in cold or damp air.          |
| 2   | Low fever               | Flushed skin, mild sweating, fatigue.             |
| 3   | Headache and eye strain | Sensitivity to light or magic auras.              |
| 4   | Skin rash or irritation | Discolored blotches or faint shimmer if arcane.   |
| 5   | Nausea                  | Stomach cramps, sour taste, queasy scent.         |
| 6   | Shortness of breath     | Especially after mild exertion.                   |
| 7   | Aching joints           | Feels like the marrow itself protests movement.   |
| 8   | Runny or bleeding nose  | Sometimes mixed with glittering motes if magical. |
| 9   | Trembling hands         | Hard to perform fine tasks.                       |
| 10  | Chills and shivering    | Even near warmth; skin goose-pimpled.             |
| 11  | Loss of appetite        | Food tastes of ash or iron.                       |
| 12  | Fatigue                 | Feels like sleep gives no rest.                   |

# Serious symptoms
|d12|Symptom|Description|
|---|---|---|
|1|Spasms or muscle locking|Limbs jerk or freeze unpredictably.|
|2|High fever|Skin hot to the touch, delirious muttering.|
|3|Bloodshot eyes|Veins darken with strange hues.|
|4|Lesions or boils|Oozing sores, possibly glowing or humming.|
|5|Dizziness and vertigo|World tilts or swims when standing.|
|6|Hallucinations|Voices, flickering shapes, phantom sensations.|
|7|Rapid heartbeat|Palpitations audible in quiet rooms.|
|8|Hair loss or brittle nails|Body visibly wasting.|
|9|Blurred or double vision|Text and sigils seem to move.|
|10|Night sweats|Soaking sheets, shivering afterward.|
|11|Coughing blood|Metallic taste and crimson spatter.|
|12|Sudden fainting|Body collapses as strength flees.|

# Life-threatening symptoms
| d12 | Symptom                | Description                                    |
| --- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Organ failure          | Jaundiced eyes, dark urine, grey skin.         |
| 2   | Uncontrolled bleeding  | Gums, nose, and pores leak sluggishly.         |
| 3   | Necrosis               | Flesh blackens and sloughs off.                |
| 4   | Paralysis              | Body stiffens; breathing shallow.              |
| 5   | Seizures               | Full-body convulsions leaving exhaustion.      |
| 6   | Unconsciousness / coma | Patient unresponsive but restless.             |
| 7   | Frosted veins          | Blood crystallizes, cold burns from within.    |
| 8   | Fever of the mind      | Memories unravel; language forgotten.          |
| 9   | Internal bursting      | Capillaries rupture under skin like ink lines. |
| 10  | Aura drain             | Magic leaks from body, leaving hollow husk.    |
| 11  | Shadow separation      | Soul flickers out of sync with flesh.          |
| 12  | Death throes           | Heart stops, eyes flare briefly, body stills.  |

# Mental symptoms
| d12 | Symptom                | Description                                                                          |
| --- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Memory gaps            | Forgets simple facts or whole hours; recent events blur together.                    |
| 2   | Paranoia               | Convinced unseen eyes are watching; hears footsteps where none exist.                |
| 3   | Emotional detachment   | Feels no joy, anger, or fear — reactions muted or delayed.                           |
| 4   | Hallucinatory whispers | Faint voices murmur names, secrets, or nonsense at the edge of hearing.              |
| 5   | Compulsion or fixation | Repeats a phrase, number, or gesture endlessly; obsesses over a meaningless detail.  |
| 6   | Disorientation         | Loses sense of direction, time, or self — forgets where or who they are momentarily. |
| 7   | Delirium               | Feverish confusion; perceives shifting patterns and impossible geometry.             |
| 8   | Skin crawling          | Feels movement under the skin; compulsively scratches or digs at it.                 |
| 9   | Unexplained bruises    | Dark marks bloom overnight in patterns like fingers or ropes.                        |
| 10  | Night terrors          | Vivid dreams indistinguishable from waking reality; wakes screaming or attacking.    |
| 11  | Desensitization        | Cuts, burns, or cold barely register — pain replaced by dull pressure.               |
| 12  | Personality fracturing | Behaviors, voice, or handwriting change suddenly as if another self takes over.      |
