This colorless, faintly sweet-smelling gas spreads rapidly when released, sinking low and filling enclosed spaces.
When released, the gas fills up to 400 meters^2 of enclosed space (to a height of 3 meters). It does not pass through solid barriers. Based on DM's ruling, some openings or certain conditions might affect the size of the area.

A breathing creature exposed to the gas must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 15) or fall unconscious after 10 seconds of exposure. Creatures holding their breath delay exposure as normal. Non-breathing creatures are unaffected. Affected creature repeats the saving throw each 10 seconds as long as they are breathing the afflicted air. An unconscious creature that remains in the gas for 2 minutes dies. A creature removed from the gas before death regains consciousness after 1d4 minutes and gains two levels of exhaustion.

The gas lingers for 30 minutes, or until dispersed by strong airflow.
