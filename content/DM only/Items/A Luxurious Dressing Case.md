*Wondrous item, common (requires attunement)*

*A silver-trimmed case containing a comb, razor, perfume vial and a tiny enchanted mirror.*

While carrying the case, you can spend 1 minute grooming yourself. Your hair and beard become perfectly styled, your skin appears refreshed, your breath is sweetened, and your clothing becomes spotless and free of unpleasant odours.
Until you finish your next long rest, you have **advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks** made to create a favourable first impression on a creature that appreciates your appearance and presentation.
The magic doesn't alter your underlying facial features, disguise your identity, or influence creatures that don't care about your appearance.
