*Wondrous item, rare*
This thumb-sized glass vial contains an inky black liquid that smells of smoke and wood. Etched around the stopper are tiny glyphs of unknown origin and meaning.

You can use its ink to draw a circle upon a solid surface you can see. Drawing the circle takes 30 seconds of uninterrupted work and consumes 1 charge of ink. The circle must fit within a 5-foot square. If the drawing is interrupted, the charge is wasted. When the sigil is completed, it completely fills with black ink and becomes a portal to a pocket archive, that remains open for the duration.

Passing through the doorway leads into a sealed room that exists apart from the surrounding world.
- *Size*: The room is a 20-foot-by-20-foot chamber with a 40-foot ceiling with a ladder in the middle. Room is furnished by many cabinets and shelfs, many of them storing books of unknown language and content.
- *Capacity*: The room can safely hold up to 8 Medium creatures, or an equivalent mix of sizes.
- *Duration:* For 1 hour, until the circle is disrupted, or until the surface bearing the circle is destroyed or heavily damaged (such as by breaking stone or burning wood).

The Pocket Archive holds **3 charges** and regains all charges daily at dawn.
