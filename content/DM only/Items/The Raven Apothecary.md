A cozy apothecary full of various ingredients including esoteric oils, salted pieces of dubious meat, common and less common alchemical herbs, and one very lively adolescent kenku named Phoebe. The back rooms contain narrow corridors and steep stairs of a common looking household furnished with dark wood furniture, walls covered with bookcases, abundance of shiny trinkets and illuminated by the shine of arcane lamps. However common the apartment might look, it is anything but ordinary. The whole space is contained in a liminal space outside of the physical realm, blessed by the ancient hands of the Domain of Death.
The apartment might be attuned to up to 3 different doors, anywhere in the physical realm. The entrance door might be connected to any of these doors, which then provide passage between the outside world and the apothecary. The attuned doors can be swapped, one at a time, with any door in the physical realm using a ritual. The ritual requires 300 gp worth of materials, has a cast time of 10 minutes and attunes the door 24 hours after casting. Once per day (at will, resets at dawn) the entrance door might be reconnected to a different attuned door or completely sealed off from the physical realm. The apartment might be freely travelled to using a Teleportation Circle with a specific sigil sequence and an item serving a key.
*Note on keys*: The appearance of keys is a flavour, completely up to you. The keys are in possession of you, Phoebe, Garrim and Sussane. You can materialize one new key per day at will.
*Note on travelling*: Each key allows you to cast a Teleportation Circle spell as a ritual with the apothecary as as destination (no material requirements, 10 minutes cast time, once per day per key).
*Note on attuned doors*: You have attuned doors at following locations:
- Doors in a narrow street in a historic centre of the *Korvain City* in the small nation of Korvain.
- Doors to a dilapidated warehouse in the outskirts of *Duskmere* in the heart of Arrkoth Empire.
- Third door *unattuned*.
