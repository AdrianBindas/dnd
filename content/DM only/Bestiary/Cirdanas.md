**Multiattack**. The githyanki makes three Longsword attacks, or it makes one of those attacks and uses Spellcasting.
**Silver Greatsword**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d6 + 7) slashing damage plus 17 (5d6) psychic damage. On a critical hit against a target in an astral body (as with the [astral projection](https://5e.tools/spells.html#astral%20projection_phb) spell), the githyanki can cut the silvery cord that tethers the target to its material body, instead of dealing damage.
