### Traits
**Magic Weapons.** Uriel's weapon attacks are magical.
**Paralyzing Aura.** As long as Uriel has at least one legendary resistance, in a 120 ft. radius every creature he wills is paralyzed. 
**Legendary Resistance** (3/Day). If Uriel fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions
**Multiattack**. The creature attacks twice with weapon.
**Claw**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 17 (2d8 + 8) slashing damage + (2d6) psychic damage.
**Twisting Words**. Uriel targets one creature he can see within 60 feet of him. The target must succeed on a DC 21 Charisma saving throw or become [charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_phb) by for 1 minute. The [charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_phb) target can repeat the saving throw if Uriel or his allies deal any damage to it. Alternatively, friendly creature can use its bonus action to try to reverse the effect (DC 21 Charisma saving throw with possible advantage/disadvantage) 
**Spellcasting**. Uriel casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20):
- At will: [Detect Thoughts](https://5e.tools/spells.html#detect%20thoughts_xphb), [Dispel Magic](https://5e.tools/spells.html#dispel%20magic_xphb)
- 2/day each: [Dimension Door](https://5e.tools/spells.html#dimension%20door_xphb), [Invisibility](https://5e.tools/spells.html#invisibility_xphb), [Scrying](https://5e.tools/spells.html#scrying_xphb)
- 1/day each: [Circle of Death](https://5e.tools/spells.html#circle%20of%20death_xphb), [Create Undead](https://5e.tools/spells.html#create%20undead_xphb)
### Reactions
**Deflect Missiles**. When the creature is hit by a ranged weapon attack, it reduces the damage from the attack by 1d10 + 9. If the damage is reduced to 0, the creature can catch the missile if it is small enough to hold in one hand and the creature has at least one hand free.
### Bonus Actions
Use Twisting Words or any spell.
### Legendary Actions
#### Legendary Actions
*Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, The creature can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The creature regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
**Frighten Foes (Costs 3 Actions).** Uriel targets up to five creatures she can see within 30 feet of her. Each target must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or be [frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_phb) of her until the end of her next turn. Any target within 5 feet of Uriel has disadvantage on the saving throw.
**Longsword Attack (Costs 2 Actions).** Uriel makes one longsword attack.
**Step into Nothing (Costs 1 Action).** Uriel, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, has the [invisible](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#invisible_phb) condition and teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet of itself. Uriel remains [invisible](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#invisible_phb) until the start of its next turn or immediately after it deals damage.
**Corrupting Guidance (Costs 1 Action)**. Uriel uses Twisting Words. Alternatively, he targets one creature [charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_phb) by him that is within 60 feet of him; that [charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_phb) target must succeed on a DC 21 Charisma saving throw, or Uriel decides how the target acts during its next turn.
