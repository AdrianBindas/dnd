**Outside**
- Small girl in plain clothing watching from the window.
**Entrance**
- The walls sound hollow, small steps of rodents can be heard.
- Creaking floor above.
- Heartbeat can be heard.
- Dripping black liquid from above.
- The wall flexes slightly as they lean on it.
- Destroying the wall causes another entrance hallway to appear.
**Living Room**
- Objects grab on to the players (dex save DC 15) and ceiling starts to fall (str DC 20).
- Can only leave using windows or a fireplace.
- Has blacksmiths hammer.
**Kids Room**
- Small girl pointing at the attic doors, where dining hall lies.
- Begs them to let her leave.
**Dining Hall**
- Half rotten, half eaten parts on the table.
- A girl's half rotten body is placed on the dining table.
- Grand dining room with 12 seats and many exits, leading to the same room. If a PC sits, a spectral version of them appears opposite and begins asking questions. Refusing to answer makes the doppelganger eat their own body parts, take 2d8 necrotic dmg. 
	- "No one escapes me...", "I will form you to my will..."
- Attempt to attack the doppelganger might cause harm to the sitting PC. Summons swarm of rats overwhelming the players, DC 20 str or con check, causing them to fall prone and suffocate (can live for 1+CON rounds).
- Doppelganger is immune to piercing damage
