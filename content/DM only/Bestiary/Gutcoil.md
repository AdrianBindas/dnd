### Traits
**Amorphous Form.** Gutcoil can move through a space as narrow as 4 inches wide without squeezing. It leaves behind a faint trail of brown, mucilaginous residue that corrodes organic matter within 1 hour.
**Cold Vulnerability.** If Gutcoil takes cold damage, it must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, its movement speed is halved and it loses its Amorphous Form trait until the end of its next turn. If it takes 20 or more cold damage in a single round, its nucleus becomes brittle - any hits against it deal double damage until the end of the creature’s next turn.
### Vulnerabilities and resistances
**Legendary resistance**: 1 per day
**Damage Vulnerabilities**: Cold
**Condition Immunities**: Prone, Charmed
### Actions
**Flail** *Melee Natural Attack*: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (3d8 + 3) slashing damage. 
**Sweep** *Melee Natural Attack*: +5 to hit, reach 15 ft., multiple targets. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) slashing damage.
**Multiattack**. The creature attacks thrice with its body (Flail, Sweep, Constrict or Crush).
**Constrict**. *Melee Natural Attack* Gutcoil coils around its victim which must make a saving throw (DC 13). On failure, victim is grappled and takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage. Gutcoil can grapple up to 3 victims at the same time.
**Crush.** *Melee Natural Attack*: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Gutcoil tightens its coils around one creature it is grappling. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or take 27 (6d8) bludgeoning damage and 10 (3d6) acid damage as its body is crushed inward.
**Roll.** Gutcoil contracts its mass and hurls itself in a tumbling motion up to 30 ft. Creatures in its path must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) bludgeoning damage and being knocked prone on a failure, or half as much on a success. Gutcoil can move through their spaces and immediately attempt to grapple one of the creatures that failed.
### Reactions
**Sudden Coil.** When a creature enters Gutcoil’s reach, it may use its reaction to make a *Constrict* attack against that creature.

**Attack** (1 point). The creature makes one Flail or Sweep attack.
#### Villain Actions

#### Lair Actions
