### Traits
3 Illusions, Perception DC 17
**Mark Prey**. As a bonus action, the Night Stalker marks one creature it can see within 120 ft.
- The Stalker always knows the target’s location.
- The target suffers disadvantage on Wisdom saves against the Stalker.
- If the target drops to 0 HP, the Stalker regains 40 HP.
**Impossible Anatomy**. The Night Stalker can move along walls and ceilings without checks and can occupy the same space as vertical surfaces.
### Vulnerabilities and resistances
**Damage Vulnerabilities**: None
**Damage Resistances**: psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities**: necrotic
**Condition Immunities**: frightened (while in dim light or darkness)
### Actions
**Multiattack**. The Night Stalker makes two/three attacks.
**Grasping Limb** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +10 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target  
**Hit:** 17 (2d10 + 6) slashing damage plus 13 (3d8) psychic damage.
If the target is the Marked Prey, it must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be Frightened until the end of its next turn.

**Crushing Embrace**. _Melee Attack:_ +10 to hit, reach 15 ft.
**Hit**: 21 (3d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage and the target is grappled (escape DC 18).
While grappled, the creature takes 14 (4d6) psychic damage at the start of its turns as whispers invade its mind.
