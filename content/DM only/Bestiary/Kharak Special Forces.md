### Traits
**Cunning Action.** On each turn in combat, the operative can use a bonus action to take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action. The operative can also use this bonus action to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
**Evasion.** If the operative is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, the operative instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if it fails.
**Blood Affinity.** When the operative deals necrotic damage to a creature, it gains temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt. These temporary hit points stack with each other.
**Uncanny Dodge (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).** The operative can use a reaction to reduce damage it takes from an attack by half, as it twists away from the blow at the last moment.

### Actions
**Multiattack.** The operative makes three blood dagger attacks or uses Crimson Bolt three times. It can replace one attack with a spell cast.
**Blood Dagger.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 8 (1d6+4) piercing damage plus 13 (3d6) necrotic damage. The operative gains temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt.
**Crimson Bolt.** _Ranged Spell Attack:_ +6 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 22 (4d8+3) necrotic damage. The operative gains temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt.
**Sorcerer Spells (5/day, DC 17, +6 to hit with spell attacks).** The operative is a 7th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 17, +6 to hit with spell attacks). The operative has the following sorcerer spells prepared:
**Cantrips (at will):** Mage Hand, Prestidigitation, Chill Touch, Mage Bolt
**1st Level (4 slots):** Magic Missile, Armor of Agathys, Scorching Ray, Expeditious Retreat, False Life
**2nd Level (3 slots):** Misty Step, Crown of Madness, Darkness, Web, Mirror Image
**3rd Level (3 slots):** Fireball, Counterspell, Haste, Blink, Vampiric Touch

### Reactions
**Shadow Step (Recharge on Long Rest).** When the operative takes damage, it can use its reaction to become invisible until the end of its next turn or until it makes an attack or casts a spell. The operative can move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks while invisible.
