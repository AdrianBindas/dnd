### Traits
**Pack Pressure.** While at least one other hunger drudge is within 5 feet of the same target, the drudge deals an extra 7 (2d6) piercing damage on a hit.
**Shriek of Pain.** When the drudge takes fire or cold damage, it emits a shriek audible out to 300 feet. Other hunger drudges that hear it have advantage on their next attack roll before the end of their next turn.
