### Traits
**Spider Climb.** The Striped can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
**Amorphous Angles.** The Striped's limbs can bend and flex in ways that defy normal anatomy. The Librarian can use its reaction to impose a disadvantage on an attack against him.

### Actions
**Multiattack.** The Striped makes three Flaying Tendril attacks or uses Bone Spray twice.
**Flaying Tendril.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 15 (3d6+4) slashing damage plus 11 (2d10) necrotic damage, and the target begins bleeding. A bleeding creature takes 5 necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns. The bleeding stacks. A creature can remove all stacks of bleed using action with a DC 12 Medicine check or by receiving magical healing. 
**Bone Spray** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 22 (4d8+4) piercing damage.
**Stripping Scream** The Striped releases an ear-piercing shriek. Each creature within 30 feet of The Striped that can hear it must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw, taking 33 (6d10) psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. Additionally, The Striped sheds crystalline dust in a 15-foot radius. Any creature that ends its turn within that radius must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 8 (2d8) slashing damage on a failed save.
### Reactions
**Boneless Dodge.** The Striped's limbs can bend and flex in ways that defy normal anatomy. The Librarian can use its reaction to impose a disadvantage on an attack against him.
### Bonus Actions
**Rampage** (1/Day). Immediately after dealing damage. the creature can move up to half its speed, and it makes one Flaying Tendril attack.
