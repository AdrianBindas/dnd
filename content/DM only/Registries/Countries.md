## Orynthia

### Arrkoth Empire

The Arrkoth Empire occupies much of western and central Orynthia and is ruled from Ellyria by Empress Teva Arrkoth and the Imperial Church. It combines mechanized industry, arcane research, military expansion, and imperial administration with corruption, discrimination, religious pressure, and growing revolt.

**Capital:** Ellyria  
**Known cities and places:** Ellyria, Sangreal, Morgenstern, Thornmarche, Maer, Blackhollow

### Aurelion League

The Aurelion League is a merchant republic governed by a triumvirate and influential across the northern waters of Orynthia. Its culture prizes coin, trade, artisans, and hired military force, allowing the old nation to remain mostly peaceful while maintaining a large mercenary presence.

**Capital:** Dravemont  
**Known cities and places:** Cinderhold, Arcadia, Emberdenn

### Iskandria

Iskandria is a small militant theocratic monarchy centered on devotion to Abaddon and the training of soldiers in his name. It employs its forces as mercenaries and conducts crusades against the Tribes, making it both a religious power and a persistent regional threat.

**Capital:** Solmira  
**Known cities and places:** Deireadh, Molgrath Fen

### The Fenmarches

The Fenmarches occupy the swampy river deltas of Orynthia, where noble families inhabit half-sunken marble mansions and peasants fish for pearls in the marshes. The realm exports black pearls and hallucinogenic frog venoms, while its people believe the fog is the breath of sleeping bog gods and tell of an undead first king.

**Capital:** Drosselburg  
**Known cities and places:** Schattenhain, Grimwald, Skuggdal, Blodtorp

### The Thalassian Queendom

The Thalassian Queendom is a matriarchal krell city-state on Orynthia's western coast, united under Queen Varys and protected by underwater cities and powerful fleets. Its people honor Xaloc, trade in rare ocean minerals, distrust surface visitors, and have preserved their independence by surviving Arrkoth expansion.

**Capital:** Coral Spire  
**Known cities and places:** Coral Spire

### The Tribes

The Tribes are a loose society of orcish families and clans in Orynthia, traditionally ruled by the strongest warchief and bound by local law, custom, and loyalty. Their recent movement toward a more rigid hierarchy has brought greater unity but also internal disputes and tension with Iskandria and Arrkoth.

**Capital or gathering place:** Isenhall  
**Known cities and places:** Isenhall, Varkuun, Nattlys

### Valdengard Territories

The Valdengard Territories are forested lands inhabited by elves, kenkus, tabaxi, and other peoples who seek a symbiotic relationship with nature. After a failed Arrkoth conquest, the region became dependent on the Empire in many matters, but resistance, sacred sites, and imperial expansion keep the peace fragile.

**Capital:** Caerwyn  
**Known cities and places:** Caerwyn, Belyndor, Brynmarrow, Loth Rakk

### Vindrik Concord

The Vindrik Concord is a northern alliance of human, dwarven, and orcish clans living among fjords, misty forests, and towering runestones. Elected jarls govern a culture of sailors and skaldic historians that must contend with both internal rivalries and a harsh climate.

**Capital:** Vinterhavn  
**Known cities and places:** Vinterhavn, Eldborg, Vargsholm, Stalgrad

### The Duchy of Redguard

The Duchy of Redguard is a fortified frontier territory within the Arrkoth sphere, ruled by the Duke from the Redguard fortress. Its royal hunts, separatist tensions, dangerous forests, occult incidents, and proximity to Loth Rakk make it one of the campaign's most contested regions.

**Seat of rule:** Redguard Fortress  
**Known cities and places:** Redguard, Highwater, Ansben, Loth Rakk

## Vesper

### The Tazrid Caliphate

The Tazrid Caliphate is a formally monarchic state in Vesper whose corruption has made it a haven for slavers, pirates, killers, and rival gangs. It was founded by exiles and slavers from across Vesper and Orynthia in inhospitable parts of the Kharak desert.

**Capital:** Korvain  
**Known cities and places:** Korvain, Nyvarra, Savena

### The House of Kharak

The House of Kharak is an ancient magocracy on the eastern coast of Vesper ruled by Elders who treat knowledge and secrecy as sources of power. Its blood magic, intelligence bureau, slavery, military research, and interest in Labyrinth studies make it a major rival to Arrkoth and the setting of the Al'Zafira arc.

**Capital:** Khar-Zuul  
**Known cities and places:** Khar-Zuul, Al'Zafira

### Bayt al-Surayya

Bayt al-Surayya is a Vesperian realm associated with arcanists, astronomers, and the House of the Pleiades. Its observatories, arcane houses, and scholarly patronage define its public identity and connect it to the wider intellectual life of Vesper.

**Political center:** House of the Pleiades  
**Known places:** Astronomical observatories, arcane academies, and scholarly estates

### The Hollow Kingdom

The Hollow Kingdom is an underground goblin alliance beneath a ruined surface city in Vesper. Its communities specialize in tunneling, smuggling, fungal agriculture, raids, and trade, while old stories claim that something demonic inhabited the caverns before humans existed.

**Capital:** The Gilded Burrow  
**Known cities and places:** The Gilded Burrow, the Obsidian Sanctum

### The Veilkin Masquerade

The Veilkin Masquerade is a hidden Vesperian enclave whose spies and assassins trade secrets throughout both continents. Its people treat identity as fluid and its true loyalties as unknowable, while its mobile capital makes the polity difficult to locate or threaten.

**Capital:** The Shifting Hall  
**Known cities and places:** The Shifting Hall, hidden waystations, and shifting safehouses

## Historical and uncertain polities

### Maer

Maer is a lost or diminished civilization whose surviving representatives seek to recover their dead and restore their former power. Its surviving communities gather around dragon-bound sites, life crystals, Balam's Iscalia, the Cult of Xolotl, and resistance to Arrkoth.

**Political center:** Maer Mountains and surviving enclaves  
**Known cities and places:** Maer Mountains, ruined archives, dragon-bound sites, and life-crystal deposits

### The Golden Republic

The Golden Republic, or República de Oro, is a wealthy republic remembered through its merchant families, ceramics, and political influence. Corazón anchors its best-known cultural tradition, while merchant houses carry its trade and diplomatic legacy across the region.

**Political center:** Corazón and the merchant houses  
**Known cities and places:** Corazón, ceramic districts, and merchant estates
