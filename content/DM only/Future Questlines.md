#### The Great Schism
A schism has befallen a great nation of Elves. Long time ago, when the humans and dwarves built their cities of stone and metal, the realms of elves were divided. Like two brothers - Elves of the Cities got accustomed and accepted the changing world. Elder Elves, as we call them, living true to the name of their fathers, sailed from the continent, seeking ancient forests still untainted by the hands of the feeble and sickly species. And so, the Elder Elves were no more.
Nowadays, elves in the cities are whispering about an eluding lands beyond the sea, children are singing rhymes of great sorcerers and druids living with the tall, ancient trees. And everyone asks the same question - what happened to the Elder Elves?
