**Race:** Veilkin (Vesper)
**Origin:** A dynasty of spies who replace their faces like changing clothes.
**Quirk:** Her true face is a shifting mosaic of everyone she’s ever impersonated.
**Profession:** Courtesan / "identity tailor" (forges new lives for fugitives).
**Goal:** To wear the face of a god—just once.
