**Dov** — encountered mid-Act III

A gnome, maybe forty years old in appearance, which could mean anything for a gnome. He arrived recently enough — maybe eight years ago — to still remember the outside clearly and talk about it constantly, which is its own kind of grief. He survived by making himself useful to the cancer: he doesn't fight it, doesn't hide from it, and in return it seems to route around him. He doesn't fully understand why. He thinks he just got lucky. He probably didn't just get lucky.

_What he knows:_ The servant's patterns. Not because he's been tracking it, but because he keeps accidentally being in places the servant was recently. He has a piece of something the servant left behind — he doesn't know what it is and has been carrying it for three years because it seemed important and he didn't know what else to do with it.

_The pity hook:_ He talks about going home constantly. He has a plan for getting out — a new plan, he's had many. They are increasingly elaborate and increasingly impossible and he knows it and keeps making them because the alternative is Sera's silence.

_The hope:_ He genuinely believes the party can do what they came to do. Not because he has evidence. Because he needs to believe it. That need is infectious in a way that is both useful and sad.

_The dread, quietly:_ The cancer routes around him. When the party has been near him a while they might notice that the ambient hostility of the Belly drops slightly. That should bother them more than it does.
