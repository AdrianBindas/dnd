**The Cartographer** — encountered in Act II or early Act III

Not humanoid. Once was — there's enough of the original shape left to suggest it — but the Belly has been changing it slowly for what might be a century. Its skin has taken on a faint translucence, veined with something that pulses faintly in the dark. It moves on four limbs now, though it clearly used to walk upright. It doesn't speak, but it communicates: it leaves markings on the walls of the Belly in a system the party can partially decode if they try. Some of the markings are maps. Some are warnings. Some are something else entirely that no one has ever been able to interpret.

_The hook:_ It is not hostile. It will follow the party at a distance for a while, which is frightening until they realize it is adding their path to its maps in real time. If they treat it carefully, it can lead them around a danger or toward the intact chamber. It has no agenda beyond the compulsion to record. Whether that compulsion was there before it arrived or the Belly gave it one is unclear.

_The dread:_ It is what happens when someone stays long enough and the Belly begins to _collaborate_ with them instead of consuming them. It is not infected. It is not hiding. It is something new. The party might look at it and think about what happens to people the entity doesn't want to consume.
