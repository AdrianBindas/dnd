- Mortal life:
	- Cursed and shunned elf orphan of unknown name in the streets of elven metropolis Summit (now in Dreamlands, connected to Glitnirdur)
	- Aims to destroy Voreth and his many priests
	- Raises a party of adventurers - Ehaageris the Cold, Nemesia, Xantholek the Mantis, K'thambr'endush.
	- Attacks Voreth's shrine and forces them to give up their beliefs.
	- He is betrayed out of envy, instead of spilling blood he surrenders and dies.
	- His conviction causes him to undergo apotheosis.
	- Bitter, tries to bring his daughter back and is punished immensely.
	- Xantholek tries to ascend the whole city but fails and all are turned to R'auleks (fall of Elves start).
- Why Hanged Man?
	- Some legends say he promised Voreth's priests no blood will be spilled if they surrender and then hung them on gallows.
	- Other say he decide to die by hanging when he was betrayed.
