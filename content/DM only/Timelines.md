Arrkoth Empire timeline:
1. Humans came to Orynthia and Vesper.
2. Humans from Vesper learned magic from Ish'karri.
3. Humans from Vesper started Arrkoth Empire.
4. Arrkoth Empire got into war with Ish'karri and lost.
5. Arrkoth Empire retreated to Orynthia.
6. Arrkoth Empire started a war on the Orynthia and won, taking control of the Ellyria.
7. Arrkoth Emperor died and gave control to his granddaughter.
8. Imperial Circle messes it up and causes revolts.
9. Arrkoth Emperor dismisses the Circle.
10. Arrkoth Emperor looks for another ways how to keep his empire and faith alive.
