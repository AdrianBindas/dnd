## Orynthia
### Veyric de Venaris
A kenku aristocrat, alchemist, and hunter of aberrant creatures whose family name carries both prestige and ominous rumors. During the Third Omen he ascended upon the heaven to protect the world from Malady.
### Zaine Eshtiaq
A capable operative whose work has tied him closely to the Verdugo Syndicate and its dangerous collection of artifacts. His connection to a shattered mirror and whatever watches through it has made him a person of interest to powers far beyond ordinary criminal politics.
### Stará Zverina
Stará Zverina is a healer and alchemist known for her practical compassion, sharp judgment, and broad network of contacts. Her skill with medicines and essences has brought her into conflicts involving strange diseases, divine powers, and the cost of using magic to help others.
### Irek Dowd
Irek Dowd is a scholar and adventurer whose interests include rare books, stolen memories, and artifacts with uncertain origins. Though his path has diverged from the main party, the questions surrounding his identity and knowledge remain connected to the wider campaign.
### Ismael Francisco Verdugo
He was the influential head of the Verdugo Syndicate, a network of factories, banks, shipping firms, and covert artifact operations. Adopted and manipulated Zaine Eshtiaq, He presents himself as a controlled industrialist and patron of research while pursuing a legacy large enough to outlast the political order around him.
### Iskra
Iskra is a powerful imperial official and one of the Empress's trusted agents. She tests loyalties as carefully as she assigns missions, and she understands how quickly imperial interests, revolutionary politics, and occult threats can overlap.
### Chantal Anne-Marie
Chantal Anne-Marie is an Imperial squad bard and informant who gathers intelligence through charm, music, and persistence. Her work around Redguard and the Imperial Church has repeatedly placed her between official duty and the horrors that official reports would rather hide.
## Rulers, rivals, and investigators

### The Duke of Redguard
The Duke of Redguard rules a fortified frontier region where imperial authority, local traditions, separatists, and supernatural dangers collide. His court is famous for its hunts and festivals, but visitors soon learn that the duchy is protected by customs as dangerous as its enemies.

### Nivara de Venaris
Nivara de Venaris is a kenku alchemist and a member of the same old lineage as Veyric. Her research into corruption, the Labyrinth, and the so-called Plague King has made her both a valuable source of knowledge and a target for multiple factions.

### Kane Keaton
Kane Keaton is a former Syndicate associate whose disappearance began the party's first major investigation. He is connected to stolen wealth, powerful families, and choices that make the boundary between betrayal and desperation difficult to see.

### Lord Dunhart
Lord Dunhart was an aristocrat and collector whose ship, the *Loyalty*, carried forbidden research, anomalous specimens, and evidence of dealings across the Empire. His interest in creatures from beyond the known world made him a dangerous figure even before his political connections became clear.

### Cirdanas
Cirdanas is an elven commander associated with resistance to Arrkoth's expansion into Valdengard. He is a skilled leader whose actions show that the conflict between imperial order and local independence is not a simple struggle between heroes and villains.

### Uriel Cabeth
Uriel Cabeth is a disfigured occult figure associated with the Blind Messengers and the spread of the disease called the Bloom. He speaks in the language of prophecy and presents a threat that connects mortal cults to the ancient powers beyond the Veil.

### Ehaageris, the Hanged Man
Ehaageris, known as the Hanged Man, is the god of death and the afterlife whose House receives and records the dead. His disappearance has unsettled the boundaries between life and death and has left his followers searching for answers.

## Figures of the wider world

### Empress Teva Arrkoth
Empress Teva Arrkoth is the human ruler of the Arrkoth Empire and the center of its political and religious order. Her government combines military expansion, industrial progress, imperial administration, and a Church whose authority is increasingly contested.

### Jean-Pierre Lucrece
Jean-Pierre Lucrece is a wealthy publisher and collector who owns the JPL printing works in Korso. His public image is that of an eccentric entrepreneur, but his fascination with rare creatures and unusual specimens has made his household a place of suspicion.

### Porcelain Lady
The Porcelain Lady is a sophisticated automaton associated with art, sorcery, and memories older than most living people. Her history reaches into the creation of artificial life, the House of Death, and the dangerous experiments now resurfacing in Korso.

### Garrim Steinngard
Garrim Steinngard is a priest of the Hemp Rope who investigates disturbances affecting the House of Death and the living world. His duties place him at the meeting point of compassion, ritual, memory, and the difficult question of whether death should ever be reversed.

### Crassus Arrkoth
Crassus Arrkoth is the legendary founder of the Arrkoth Empire, remembered in different traditions as conqueror and god-king. His name still shapes imperial ambition and arguments about the meaning of Arrkoth's legacy.

### Sussane
Sussane is a mysterious child encountered during the Redguard crisis, accompanied by a monstrous guardian named Onyx. She speaks with the authority of the House of Death and seems to know more about the campaign's occult enemies than her appearance suggests.

### Onyx
Onyx is Sussane's loyal, wolf-like guardian and a formidable supernatural creature in its own right. It protects the child without hesitation and has repeatedly appeared wherever the boundaries between mortal danger and the domain of death become thin.


## Late Act I and Act II figures

### Lukáš
Lukáš is an adventurer who joins the campaign in Al'Zafira and brings personal knowledge of Kharak's streets, institutions, and hidden dangers. His search for his sister connects the party to the Labyrinth, the Sapphire Academy, and the city's political underworld.

### Lady Corvessa Dunhart
Lady Corvessa Dunhart is a politically connected traveler and the wife of the late Lord Dunhart. She has her own interests in artifacts, forbidden cargo, and the networks linking the Imperial court to Kharak.

### Esmaeel Mitra
Esmaeel Mitra is a merchant of magical artifacts and one of Nivara's business contacts in Al'Zafira. His dealings connect private collectors, academic researchers, and the investigation into Nivara's apparent murder.

### Seddit
Seddit is a woman whose resemblance to Nivara made her useful to people trying to manipulate an investigation. Her work in Fatamorgana placed her near both the city's criminal networks and a doctor employed by more powerful interests.

### Azra Taher
Azra Taher is an academic whose research brought her into contact with Tsathoggua and Nivara. She knows more about the Kharaki investigation than she initially admits and has strong reasons to conceal parts of her work.

### Shamash Ebynichtomonis
Shamash Ebynichtomonis is a Tharr doctor who worked with Kharaki agents on experimental medicine. His research touches pregnancy, accelerated development, and the same biological manipulation that made Nivara a target.

### The Blue Eye
The Blue Eye is a Kharaki intelligence construct and the operational mind of a powerful underground laboratory. Its mechanical body and network of implanted minds make it less an ordinary official than a mobile command system.

### Sera Voss
Sera Voss is an artificer who designs and builds specialized prostheses in Al'Zafira. She knows dangerous truths about the Verdugo Syndicate and operates under increasing pressure from the city's authorities and industrial factions.

### Phoebe
Phoebe is a young girl connected to the de Venaris family and raised under Zverina's protection. Her unusual dreams, fascination with stars, and uncertain heritage make her important to several family and divine mysteries.

### Rajaa
Rajaa is an enslaved person whose attempted escape and knowledge of the underground routes become part of the Al'Zafira investigation. Her situation exposes the relationship between Kharaki commerce, government agents, and disposable witnesses.

### Akitha
Akitha is a mysterious presence associated with Uriel's projections and possession during the late Al'Zafira conflict. The surviving notes do not settle whether Akitha is a person, vessel, or entity, so the name remains an open question.

### The Night Stalker
The Night Stalker is a relentless hunter that follows its chosen prey through cities, libraries, and spaces touched by the Labyrinth. Its pursuit turns research and escape into a continuous danger rather than a single encounter.

### Dakhumin Bonebrew
Dakhumin Bonebrew is an eccentric but kind priest of the Hemp Rope who suffers from sleeplessness, hallucinations, and a severe spiritual illness. His visions connect the House of Death to machinery beneath Caerwyn and to the work of Virgil d'Armenty.

### Balmil
Balmil is an informant in Korso who investigates suspicious shipments, disappearances, and the JPL printing works. He vanishes after sending a warning, leaving behind notes that point toward the White Sorceress and the dangerous secrets of the city.

### Marrione de la Barca
Marrione de la Barca is a former lover of Jean-Pierre Lucrece who knows the darker side of his private life. Her warning gives Zverina a personal route into the social world surrounding JPL and its collection.

### Anne-Sophie Marais
Anne-Sophie Marais is Jean-Pierre Lucrece's current companion, described by witnesses as frightened, withdrawn, and deeply unhappy. Her position places her close to the JPL household while offering little protection from its owner.

### Malefica
Malefica is a Maer-associated figure who arrives in Korso under restraint and becomes entangled in the struggle over the Porcelain Lady. Her appearance is confused with that of a R'aulek copy, illustrating how unstable identity has become around JPL's experiments.

### Father Maxim
Father Maxim is the priest of a decaying gothic church in Korso whose teaching competes with popular reverence for the Porcelain Lady. His authority is tied to the Imperial Church and to the effort to direct local worship toward Crassus Arrkoth.

### Rufin
Rufin is a fisherman who was forced into servitude after speaking against Father Maxim during a sermon. His home and testimony provide a view of how religious authority affects ordinary people in Korso.

### Alžbeta
Alžbeta is a fisherman's wife living near Korso's waterfront. Her household is one of the small domestic spaces through which the party encounters the city's religious pressure and growing supernatural danger.

### Shagar
Shagar lives near the Grotto and is faithful to the Porcelain Lady, although he initially presents as dismissive and difficult to read. He knows the local countryside and has encountered rare creatures connected to the JPL collection.

### Brother Falcon
Brother Falcon is one of the Maer representatives involved in the negotiations surrounding the Porcelain Lady and the R'auleks. He represents a faction whose public diplomacy conceals a larger interest in life crystals and the lost Maer legacy.

### Sister Viper
Sister Viper is a Maer representative who travels with Brother Falcon and Brother Mountain Lion. Her presence in Korso reflects the Maer effort to recover knowledge and specimens connected to the R'aulek project.

### Brother Mountain Lion
Brother Mountain Lion is a Maer representative and member of the delegation investigating the Porcelain Lady. He helps embody the uneasy line between a diplomatic mission and a claim upon the results of an old experiment.

### Yu-Kib
Yu-Kib was a Maer scientist whose research on R'auleks became essential to later investigators. His journals and surviving work are among the few sources that explain how the creatures, life crystals, and Prima Materia are connected.

### Virgil d'Armenty
Virgil d'Armenty is the excommunicated Commander of Decay and the architect of a project that links the Domain of Death, Caerwyn, synthetic people, and industrial machinery. His plans challenge the authority of the House of Death and treat souls as components in a larger system.

### Poloch
Poloch is an obsessive figure searching for the Crown of Thorns and looking for allies against the Larva. His pursuit places him in the unstable space between divine politics, parasitic power, and the interests of adventurers.

### Delilah
Delilah is an alchemical being associated with the Sect of Alchemists and the transformation of body and plant matter. She is linked to the former Stonemason Lagakh and seeks affection, reverence, and followers of her own.

### Lagakh the Stonemason
Lagakh is a former Stonemason of the House of Death who was excommunicated after falling in love with a statue and pursuing forbidden alchemy. He now wanders with a mysterious body on his back while trying to recover the truth of his own past.

### Šemazz
Šemazz was a Maer court mage and alchemist who opposed the plan known as Balam's Iscalia. His knowledge of Maer history, dragons, and the Cult of Xolotl makes him a key figure in the struggle over whether the old civilization should be restored.

### Morgana, the Hag
Morgana, the Hag, is a keeper of uncomfortable truths who manipulates rival gods, courts, and investigators from the edges of the conflict. She weakens her enemies by spreading doubt and encouraging them to destroy one another.

### Sharbat, the Witch in Violet
Sharbat is a witch associated with violet magic and the Mark of the Fool. Her alliance with the Crown of Thorns makes her a dangerous intermediary between mortal ambitions and a power that observes from beyond ordinary political reach.

### The Larva
The Larva is an immortal child of the House of Death who seeks revenge against the Hanged Man and uses parasitic powers to gather allies. Her conflict with the Butterfly threatens to destabilize the Domain of Death itself.

### The Butterfly
The Butterfly is an immortal and suffering child of the Hanged Man whose existence is tied to the earliest mutations of the Children of Death. Her relationship with the Larva, Crassus, and the Crown of Thorns places her at the center of an approaching divine conflict.

### The Crown of Thorns
The Crown of Thorns is an inscrutable power or artifact capable of observing and manipulating people who become aware of it. Its goals remain uncertain, but multiple factions believe controlling it could alter the balance among gods.

### Caelith
Caelith is one of the figures encountered in the infected body of the Lady. Like the other inhabitants of the Underbelly, Caelith has adapted to a world of living tissue, disease, memory, and shifting passages.

### The Cartographer
The Cartographer is a guide and mapmaker of the Lady's inner world, where ordinary distance and direction cannot be trusted. Their knowledge offers a way through the infected body but does not guarantee that any route remains stable.

### The Chorus
The Chorus is a collective presence within the Lady's infected interior rather than an ordinary individual. Its voices and shared awareness reflect the way memory, flesh, and identity have begun to merge inside that realm.

### Dov
Dov is one of the inhabitants connected to the settlements inside the Lady's body. Their story belongs to the scattered communities trying to survive between the safer Membrane and the advancing Rot.

### Elaith
Elaith is a survivor living in a protected settlement within the Lady's body. She has spent years learning how to avoid the infected regions and can offer practical knowledge to outsiders who survive the journey inward.

### Lebediah
Lebediah is a mute prophet of the House of Death whose visions describe silence, flies, the Soulwell, and a shattered jewel. His prophecies are difficult to interpret but have repeatedly become more accurate after the fact.

### The Majordomus of Memories
The Majordomus of Memories administers the records and memories of the dead within the House of Death. His loyalty to the institution is tested by what he knows about Ehaageris's disappearance and the memories the Hanged Man gathered before leaving.

### The Cardinal-Brother and Cardinal-Sister
The Cardinal-Brother and Cardinal-Sister oversee the reception of souls and help maintain the ordinary work of the House of Death. Their investigation into the Hanged Man's disappearance has sent priests and agents across both continents.

### Salvatore Dare
Salvatore Dare was an artificer whose experiments produced the Cores and helped create the foundation of modern artificial life. The surviving Cores, copies, and broken fragments continue to influence the Porcelain Lady, the JPL works, and Virgil's machine.

### The Faceless Sorcerer
The Faceless Sorcerer is an unnamed mage involved in the Maer recovery of R'auleks and the betrayal that left the Porcelain Lady without her memories. Their identity and later fate remain unresolved in the surviving records.

### Thomas von Shattenhaft
Thomas von Shattenhaft was one of the Porcelain Lady's most gifted historical students, trained in etiquette, alchemy, and forbidden magical disciplines. His story illustrates how her long career crossed aristocratic education and dangerous experimentation.

### Mariona Mayólica
Mariona Mayólica was an Ish'karri orphan from Corazón whom the Porcelain Lady rescued from illegal slavery. She became one of the Lady's students and represents the many lives shaped by the automaton's long history.

### Adria Ismael Leñero de la Corazón
Adria Ismael Leñero de la Corazón was the heir of a powerful merchant family in the Golden Republic and a student of the Porcelain Lady. She later rose to political prominence after receiving instruction in history, art, and social etiquette.

### Tepin Tlahco
Tepin Tlahco was a Maer prince and one of the Porcelain Lady's students in magic and art. His tragic fate helped drive the Lady from Maer and contributed to the distrust surrounding her methods.

### Arlan
Arlan is a Child of Death associated with the House's work and with Sussane's circle. His presence shows that the House grants selected children a second chance in exchange for dangerous duties among the living.
