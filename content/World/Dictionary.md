## Cosmology and arcana
### Ascendants and Ascendancy
Ascendants are beings or individuals who have reached enough power to move between the material world and the Labyrinth. Ascendancy is understood as transformation, apotheosis, or elevation beyond ordinary life, although different cultures disagree about whether it is a blessing, a danger, or both.
### The Labyrinth
The Labyrinth is the vast and dangerous reality surrounding the material World, containing other worlds, strange branches, and paths where ordinary distance does not behave normally. It is the source or conduit of much magic, and explorers who enter it risk encountering places, beings, and laws unlike anything on the surface.

### The Dreamlands and the Veil

The Dreamlands are the region closest to the material World, while the Veil is the thin boundary separating them from the deeper Labyrinth. The Veil is especially fragile around Orynthia and Vesper, allowing dreams, visions, spirits, and distant powers to cross more easily.

### Spirits

Spirits are souls, remnants, or concentrations of energy that remain connected to the physical world. They may inhabit places, objects, memories, or living beings, and their behavior ranges from protective and ancestral to hostile and impossible to understand.

### Gods and Worship

Gods are powerful beings approached through prayer, worship, sacrifice, tradition, and the institutions built in their names. Different peoples recognize different divine patrons, and the boundary between god, spirit, Ascendant, and ancient power is not interpreted the same way everywhere.

### Primordials

Primordials are ancient powers associated with the creation and earliest history of the World. Stories place them before the modern gods and common peoples, but surviving accounts disagree about their identities, motives, and whether their age has truly ended.

### Aberrant Beings

Aberrant beings are creatures whose forms, instincts, or origins do not fit the ordinary life of the material World. Many are associated with the Labyrinth, dimensional breaches, or ancient infestations, and their presence often brings disease, mutation, imitation, or madness.

### Dimensional Rifts

Dimensional rifts are tears or openings through the Veil and the Labyrinth that connect places normally separated by impossible distances. They may be created by powerful magic, artifacts, celestial events, or beings on the other side, and they are rarely safe in either direction.

## Diseases and transformations

### The Bloom

The Bloom is a spreading magical disease associated with corruption, infection, and abnormal growth. It is also called the Withering Bloom or Violet Bloom in different records, and its symptoms can affect flesh, plants, animals, minds, and entire regions.

### The Malady

The Malady is a name used for a vast force of disease and ruin, also called the Emperor of All Maladies or the Plague King by different witnesses. It is linked to the Bloom and to the return of ancient powers, but even scholars disagree on whether the name describes a creature, an infection, a god, or an idea that has learned to spread.

### Blood Magic

Blood magic uses life force as a source, medium, or price for supernatural effects. It is strongly associated with Ish'karri traditions and Kharaki research, but practitioners across the world have developed their own rituals, taboos, and ways to survive its costs.

## Artifacts and living technology

### Life Crystals and Prima Materia

Life crystals are rare sources of concentrated vitality that can preserve, empower, or transform living and artificial things. Prima Materia is a related term for especially potent life essence, and both are sought by alchemists, Maer survivors, artificers, and factions trying to overcome the limits of death.

### Grimwald Mirrors

Grimwald Mirrors are ancient reflective artifacts that can reveal places, beings, or perspectives beyond an ordinary reflection. Broken mirrors remain dangerous because their shards may preserve a connection, alter the observer, or allow something distant to look back.

### Cores and Automatons

A Core is an arcane heart capable of powering a construct, preserving a mind, or imitating parts of life. Automatons are living constructs whose bodies and purposes vary widely, and the existence of independent consciousness has made their rights, origins, and relationship with their creators a major social question.

### R'auleks

R'auleks are strange beings associated with old Maer research, transformation, and the production of life crystals. Their bodies and identities can change in disturbing ways, making them central to debates about imitation, personhood, artificial life, and the limits of experimentation.

### Arcane Industry

Arcane industry is the union of magic, engineering, manufacturing, and mechanized infrastructure that defines the modern cities of Orynthia and Vesper. It powers railways, printing presses, universities, weapons, automatons, and laboratories while also creating new forms of exploitation, pollution, surveillance, and social unrest.

## Institutions and movements

### The House of Death

The House of Death is both a divine institution and a realm responsible for receiving, recording, and guiding the dead. Its priests, Cardinals, Lightkeepers, Stonemasons, Children of Death, and other offices uphold the principle that death is a passage all must face and should not be reversed without cause.

### The Imperial Church

The Imperial Church is the religious and administrative institution that supports the Arrkoth Empire. It provides education, protection, taxation, and proselytization, but its growing authority, censorship, treatment of foreign faiths, and involvement in arcane research have made it a focus of resistance.

### The Blind Messengers

The Blind Messengers are an occult movement whose followers appear at scenes of disease, disappearance, and ritual violence. They speak of an approaching swarm or ending and use symbols associated with eyes, hands, roots, and hidden powers beyond the ordinary world.

### Marks of the Fool

The Mark of the Fool is an unusual sign associated with chance, luck, risk, and the unpredictable god known as the Fool or Gambler. Those who bear it may be treated as blessed, cursed, manipulated, or simply dangerous, depending on the culture and the event surrounding the mark.
