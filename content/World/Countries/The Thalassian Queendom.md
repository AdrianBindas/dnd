**Location**: Orynthia (western coast)
**Capital**: Coral Spire
**Society**: Matriarchal krell city-state united under Queen Varys. They trade rare ocean minerals but fiercely guard their underwater cities.
**Culture**: Rituals honor Xaloc, the ocean god. Surface visitors are rare and distrusted.
**History**: Survived Arrkoth's expansion by sinking their fleets. Nowadays their ally and a powerhouse of marine trade and economy.
**Cities and places**: None except Coral Spire
