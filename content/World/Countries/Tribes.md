**Location**: Orynthia
**Capital**: Isenhall
**Society**: Countless families and tribes of orcs, in a loosely hierarchical society. Ruled by the strongest warchief.
**Culture**: Many devoted to Uriel, protectors of peace (as long as they are not fighting against each other).
**History**: Previously barbaric, lately turned to more rigid society through the effort of multiple strong tribes.
**Cities and places**: Varkuun, Nattlys
