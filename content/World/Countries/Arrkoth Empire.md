**Location**: Western and central Orynthia
**Capital**: Ellyria
**Society**: A militaristic theocracy ruled by Empress Teva Arrkoth, where the Imperial Church enforces doctrine and collects taxes. The empire thrives on mechanized industry and arcane research, but corruption and discrimination fester beneath its gleaming cities.
**Culture**: Humans dominate the aristocracy, while other races face systemic oppression. The Church venerates the Empress as a divine figure, blending faith with state control.
**History**: Founded by the ruling Arrkoth bloodline, the empire conquered Orynthia through war and diplomacy. Recent revolts and the Bloodshed at Absolution Square have weakened its grip.
**Cities and places**: Ellyria, Sangreal, Morgenstern, Thornmarche, Maer, Blackhollow
