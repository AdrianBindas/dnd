**Location**: Vesper (underground, beneath a ruined surface city)
**Capital**: The Gilded Burrow (a cavern lit by bioluminescent fungi)
**Society**: A goblin alliance. They specialize in tunneling, smuggling, fungal agriculture and raids.
**Culture**: Mostly raiders and smugglers but also merchants and adventures looking for better life somewhere else.
**History**: The caverns are said to be inhabited before humans even existed. Something demonical was calling those caverns it's home.
