**Location**: Orynthia (swampy river deltas)
**Capital**: Drosselburg
**Society**: A crumbling feudal state where noble families live in half-sunken marble mansions, arguing over titles while peasants fish for pearls in the muck. Exports rare black pearls and hallucinogenic frog venoms.
**Culture**: Believe fog is the breath of sleeping bog gods.
**History**: Founded by refugees from Arrkoth's early conquests who "disappeared" into the marshes. Legend says their first king still rules as an undead.
**Cities and places**: Schattenhain, Grimwald, Skuggdal, Blodtorp
