- **Location**: Orynthia
- **Size:** Large coastal city
- **Description:** A fog-shrouded port where the sun never fully rises, and the streets are lit by bioluminescent jellyfish lanterns.
- **Unique Feature:** The "Tidegrave," a submerged district that surfaces only at midnight, revealing its waterlogged secrets.
- **Common Trait:** A thriving smuggling ring dealing in "drowned wine" (aged in sunken barrels).
