- **Location**: Vesper
- **Size:** Medium-sized scholarly enclave
- **Description:** A city of translucent domes where alchemists grow gemstone flora and crystal livestock. The streets are paved with tempered rainbow glass.
- **Common Trait:** A guild of "Glassblades" who duel with swords that shatter and reform mid-battle.
