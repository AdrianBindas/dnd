- **Location**: Orynthia
- **Size:** Small mining town
- **Description:** A soot-stained village built into the side of a collapsed dwarven mine. The streets are lit by glowing fungi, and the air smells of sulfur and damp stone.
- **Unique Feature:** The "Weeping Shaft"—a mine that echoes with the voices of long-dead miners. Some say it whispers prophecies.
- **Common Trait:** A rough-and-tumble tavern, _The Pick and Ghost_, where miners bet their earnings on underground beast fights.
