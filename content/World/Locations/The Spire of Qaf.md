- **Location**: Vesper
- **Size:** Towering metropolis
- **Description:** A vertical city carved into a colossal obsidian mountain, where nobles live at the sunlit peak and the poor dwell in the "Rootwarrens" below.
- **Unique Feature:** The "Weeping Shaft"—a mine that echoes with the voices of long-dead miners. Some say it whispers prophecies.
- **Common Trait:** A guild of "Skybeggars" who dangle from ropes to ask for alms from higher tiers.
