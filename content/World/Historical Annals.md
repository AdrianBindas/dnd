# Important events registry

Dates in Orynthia and Vesper are not always recorded consistently, so this list favors sequence and shared significance over a single disputed calendar. Recent events are described from the information available to the public and the adventurers.

## Deep history

### The Age of Primordials
Ancient traditions speak of powerful beings known as Primordials who shaped the World from within the Labyrinth before the present nations. Their remnants, descendants, and old rivalries are invoked to explain the Veil, the gods, and creatures that return from beyond it.

### The departure of the elves
Thousands of years ago, the great elven civilization withdrew or disappeared from the surface world, beginning the long rise of human powers in Orynthia and Vesper. Ruins, surviving traditions, and unanswered questions about the departure remain scattered across the continents.

### The burning of Demasae
About seven hundred years ago, the burning of Demasae became a turning point in Orynthian history and was associated with the ascendance of Zelios. The event is remembered as part of the transition from older elven and primordial legacies toward the age of fractured kingdoms.

### The Kharak arcane revolution
Several centuries ago, Kharak transformed from a militaristic power that rejected forbidden magic into a magocracy built around magical research and merit. The change reshaped Vesper, separated Tazrid from Kharak's former sphere, and established the rivalry that now threatens to become open war with Arrkoth.

### The rise of Abaddon
About four hundred years ago, Abaddon's influence rose across Orynthia and Vesper as a major force associated with protection, justice, and order. His churches and followers remain important to both local communities and the political struggles surrounding imperial religion.

### The Arrkoth conquest
The Arrkoth dynasty expanded its power across Orynthia through conquest, diplomacy, and imperial administration. The exact founding date and relationship between the dynasty's Vesperian origins and its later Orynthian empire remain disputed, but its expansion created the political map of the current age.

## Recent history and campaign events

### Bloodshed at the Absolution Square
A night of protests, violence, and an unknown disease isolated part of Ellyria behind a powerful barrier. The incident became a defining confrontation between the Imperial Church, non-human communities, and the adventurers who survived it, and it serves as the prelude to the current campaign.

### The attack on Ismael Verdugo
An assassination attempt against Ismael Francisco Verdugo drew the adventurers into the internal politics of the Verdugo Syndicate. It also revealed that imperial, revolutionary, Kharaki, and occult interests were all circling the Syndicate's artifacts and research.

### The fall of the meteorite
A strange object fell near the borders of Valdengard and Redguard, creating concern among astronomers, the Imperial Church, the Duke, and several rival factions. The object was eventually associated with dimensional rifts, anomalous life, and the ruins of Loth Rakk.

### The Ansben catastrophe
During a royal hunt in Redguard, the party found Ansben transformed by a ritual linked to the Blind Messengers. The catastrophe turned a distant rumor about an occult movement into a direct threat to the duchy and everyone traveling through it.

### The Loth Rakk expedition
The adventurers entered Loth Rakk, an ancient elven complex being used for dangerous research into creatures, dimensions, and the fallen object. What they found connected the Imperial Church, the Bloom, Uriel Cabeth, the Blind Messengers, and powers beyond the Veil.

### Uriel Cabeth's assault on Redguard
Uriel Cabeth attacked the forces gathered beneath Redguard and overcame the Duke's defenses. The battle exposed the scale of the threat behind the Bloom and forced the survivors, the Empire, and the Syndicate to reconsider their understanding of the enemy.

### The Verdugo succession crisis
After Ismael Verdugo's death or removal from power, the Syndicate's factions began maneuvering to control its companies, agents, and artifact vaults. The resulting succession struggle made personal loyalties as important as political alliances and pulled the party deeper into Ellyrian intrigue.

### Nivara's abduction in Al'Zafira
Nivara de Venaris traveled to Al'Zafira while investigating the Plague King, the Labyrinth, and possible ways to stop the spreading Bloom. Her apparent murder became an abduction connected to Kharaki research, forcing the party into the city's academies, hidden laboratories, and political underworld.

### The disappearance of the Hanged Man
Ehaageris, the Hanged Man and god of death, vanished from the seat of his House, leaving his followers to investigate a crisis that reaches beyond the afterlife. The disappearance weakened the boundaries and institutions that normally separate the living, the dead, and those who would exploit both.

### The Korso investigations
In Korso, Garrim Steinngard and Stará Zverina began investigating disappearances, poisoned food, strange creatures, and the troubled JPL printing works. Their inquiries point toward the Porcelain Lady, the R'auleks, Maer interests, and a machine being assembled somewhere beneath the ordinary world.
