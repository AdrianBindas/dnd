Arrkoth Empire timeline:
1. Humans came to Orynthia and Vesper.
2. Humans from Vesper learned magic from Ish'karri.
3. Humans from Vesper started Arrkoth Empire.
4. Arrkoth Empire got into war with Ish'karri and lost.
5. Year 1 of the Reign of the Empire: Arrkoth Empire retreated to Orynthia.
6. Year 3 of the Reign of the Empire: Arrkoth Empire started a war on the Orynthia and won, taking control of the Ellyria.
7. Year 56 of the Reign of the Empire: Arrkoth Emperor died and gave control to his descendants.
8. Year 340 of the Reign of the Empire: Bloodshed at the Absolution Square - Order messes it up and causes revolts.
9. Arrkoth Empress Teva dismisses the Order.
10. Arrkoth Empress Teva looks for another ways how to keep his empire and faith alive.
