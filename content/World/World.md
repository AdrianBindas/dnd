In a place where technology and arcane intertwine, where gods meddle in the affairs of mortals, where nations rise and fall and far-away lands are discovered, your adventures take place. 

The known world composes of two continents - Orynthia on the East and Vesper on the West. Both continents are populated by myriad of species - some are more Tolkien-esque (many times with a twist), others are popular hybrids of humans and animals while the rest is exotic and outlandish.
Their fates intertwine, many face discrimination or prejudice, other have unified and built large empires, while others have already fallen.

Massive cities climb to the skies thanks to power mages and skilled mechanists. Famous universities in the Orynthia study not only arcane but also engineering, smithing, enchanting and other crafts. Faraway places still harbor ancient magic and eldritch beasts, out of sight of hunters or scholars.

The continent of Orynthia is a home to an Orynthian Empire - ruled by the Empress who has already spread hers influence over most of the continent, bringing progress, wealth and unity. But this colossal war machine is beginning to rot - corruption, exploitation and discrimination all threaten its existence from the inside. 

In other parts of the world, enemies of the empire scheme and conspire, depraved monsters hunt freely and ancient things return from the oblivion.

It is now, that you shall take charge of your destiny and shape the world to your will.
