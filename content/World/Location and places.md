# Places registry

This registry is a quick orientation guide to places that shape the setting and the campaign. Descriptions focus on what travelers, scholars, and ordinary citizens might reasonably know; deeper secrets remain in the DM notes.

## Continents

### Orynthia
Orynthia is the eastern continent, home to the Arrkoth Empire and many older nations, cultures, and sacred wilds. Its cities combine arcane research and mechanized industry with imperial expansion, religious conflict, and unrest.

### Vesper
Vesper is the western continent, separated from Orynthia by the high seas and connected to it by trade, politics, and the Labyrinth. Its regions include the House of Kharak, desert powers, hidden enclaves, and cities shaped by older and stranger traditions.

## Cities and sites

### The Grand City of Ellyria
Ellyria is the capital of the Arrkoth Empire and one of the greatest cities in the known world. Its layered districts contain palaces, universities, factories, mines, markets, temples, slums, and communities shaped by many different peoples.

### Redguard Fortress
The Redguard Fortress is the seat of the Duke and the center of the duchy's military and ceremonial life. Its court, underground passages, royal hunts, and connection to the surrounding forests have made it a focal point for imperial, occult, and separatist conflicts.

### Highwater
Highwater is a coastal settlement and port connected to Redguard by trade and politics. Its docks, aristocratic estates, and private collections make it a useful place to find ships, contraband, rumors, and trouble.

### Loth Rakk
Loth Rakk is an ancient elven ruin in the Valdengard region, originally associated with worship of the Lady. In the present age it is also remembered as a site of imperial research, dangerous relics, and encounters with beings from beyond the ordinary world.

### Ansben
Ansben was a small lakeside village encountered during the Redguard royal hunt. The settlement became the scene of a devastating occult incident that brought the threat of the Blind Messengers into sharp focus.

### Al'Zafira
Al'Zafira is a luxurious oasis city on the edge of Kharak, famous for scholarship, pleasure, trade, and political intrigue. Its Sapphire Academy studies theoretical magic and the Labyrinth, while its hidden networks attract spies, fugitives, and ambitious researchers.

### Korso
Korso is a small coastal city whose recent prominence comes from the JPL printing works and its eccentric owner, Jean-Pierre Lucrece. Its streets connect journalism, commerce, religious tension, exotic collections, and the unsettling remains of older experiments.

### Caerwyn
Caerwyn is a major city and regional center associated with the Valdengard Territories and their uneasy relationship with Arrkoth. Its public institutions, forests, and old catacombs preserve layers of history that continue to attract scholars, artificers, and dangerous visitors.

### Blackhollow
Blackhollow is a soot-stained mining town built into the remains of a collapsed dwarven mine. Its glowing fungi, sulfurous air, and the haunted Weeping Shaft have made it a place where miners and adventurers learn to respect what lies below.

### The Drowning Library
The Drowning Library is a submerged archive in a black lake, where waterlogged magic preserves books and turns ink into temporary prophecies. Its librarian is said to trade answers for memories, making every visit an exchange rather than a simple act of research.

### The Spire of Qaf
The Spire of Qaf is a towering city carved into an obsidian mountain in Vesper. Its bright upper levels and impoverished Rootwarrens below reflect the continent's sharp divisions of wealth, power, and access to magic.


### Al’Miraj
Al’Miraj is a bustling desert port built around the massive skeletal ribcage of an ancient leviathan. Ships dock between the bones while markets, travelers, and the trade routes of Vesper gather in its shadow.

### Duskwatch
Duskwatch is a fog-shrouded coastal city where the sun never fully rises and bioluminescent jellyfish illuminate the streets. Its submerged Tidegrave district surfaces at midnight, while smugglers deal in wine aged in drowned barrels.

### The Bone Orchard
The Bone Orchard is a forest whose trees grow from buried warriors and whose branches resemble skeletal hands. Its ivory-hard fruit carries whispers of battle, and the oldest tree is said to preserve a fallen hero's memories and unfinished vengeance.

### The Glass Gardens of Vith
The Glass Gardens of Vith are a scholarly enclave of translucent domes, gemstone flora, and crystal livestock. Alchemists and Glassblades fill its rainbow-glass streets, making the city both a center of unusual research and a place where duels can become demonstrations.



### Sangreal
Sangreal is one of the named cities of the Arrkoth Empire in western and central Orynthia. Its place in the imperial network is recorded, but the World notes do not yet specify a distinctive local culture or landmark.

### Morgenstern
Morgenstern is an Arrkoth city associated with the Empire's expanding urban and administrative network. Its avenues, offices, and workshops reflect the imperial blend of bureaucracy, industry, and arcane progress.

### Thornmarche
Thornmarche is a named city or territory of the Arrkoth Empire. Its frontier position places it among the imperial settlements where military administration, trade, and local customs meet.

### Dravemont
Dravemont is the capital of the Aurelion League and the seat of its merchant triumvirate. Its position over the northern waters makes it a natural center for trade, mercenaries, and maritime politics.

### Cinderhold
Cinderhold is a named city of the Aurelion League. Its merchants, artisans, and mercenary contracts tie it to the League's northern commercial sphere.

### Arcadia
Arcadia is a named city of the Aurelion League. Its workshops, counting houses, and guild connections place it within the merchant republic's network of cities and artisans.

### Emberdenn
Emberdenn is a named city of the Aurelion League. Its trade houses and river or coastal connections support Aurelion's northern commerce and mercenary economy.

### Solmira
Solmira is the capital of Iskandria and the center of its militant devotion to Abaddon. It is likely to be closely tied to the blessed statue and the military institutions that define the small theocratic monarchy.

### Deireadh
Deireadh is a city of Iskandria and part of the realm's network of soldiers, mercenaries, and religious institutions. Its barracks, shrines, and training grounds reflect Iskandria's devotion to Abaddon and its crusading ambitions.

### Molgrath Fen
Molgrath Fen is an Iskandrian place named in connection with the country's crusading and regional conflicts. It is also associated with an earlier Blind Messenger incident, giving it significance beyond the country's ordinary political geography.

### Drosselburg
Drosselburg is the capital of the Fenmarches, surrounded by swampy river deltas and noble estates. It is the political center of a realm whose wealth comes from black pearls, fishing, and hallucinogenic frog venoms.

### Schattenhain
Schattenhain is a city of the Fenmarches. Its name and setting place it within the fog-shrouded marsh culture of noble houses, drowned roads, and stories about bog gods.

### Grimwald
Grimwald is a Fenmarches settlement associated with old mirrors, family curses, and stories of Azathoth. It is one of the places where the boundary between local folklore and the wider cosmic mysteries becomes difficult to separate.

### Skuggdal
Skuggdal is a named city or settlement of the Fenmarches. Its streets and waterways wind through fog, reed beds, and the half-sunken estates characteristic of the marsh realm.

### Blodtorp
Blodtorp is a named settlement of the Fenmarches. Its people live among flooded roads, pearl fisheries, and noble claims that are as unstable as the marsh beneath them.

### Korvain
Korvain is the capital of the Tazrid Caliphate and a major Vesperian center of corruption, piracy, and slaving networks. The city anchors the Caliphate's formal monarchy even though much of its practical power lies with gangs and private interests.

### Nyvarra
Nyvarra is a city of the Tazrid Caliphate where desert routes meet the Caliphate's maritime trade. Its markets carry goods, rumors, and illicit contracts between pirates, slavers, and traveling merchants.

### Savena
Savena is a named city of the Tazrid Caliphate. Its neighborhoods are shaped by gang power, caravan trade, and the uneasy authority of a distant formal monarchy.

### The Gilded Burrow
The Gilded Burrow is the capital of the Hollow Kingdom, built in a cavern lit by bioluminescent fungi. It is a center of goblin alliance, tunneling, fungal agriculture, smuggling, and trade beneath the ruined surface world.

### The Obsidian Sanctum
The Obsidian Sanctum is a fortified black-stone complex within the Hollow Kingdom. It serves as a meeting place for goblin clans, tunnel guides, and merchants who move between the underground settlements.

### Khar-Zuul
Khar-Zuul is the capital of the House of Kharak and the seat of the Elders. It is the political center from which Kharak directs its blood magic, intelligence operations, secrecy, and competition with Arrkoth.

### Coral Spire
Coral Spire is the capital and principal city of the Thalassian Queendom. Built around the krell's maritime and underwater society, it is a center of ocean trade, Xaloc's worship, and guarded diplomacy with the surface.

### The Shifting Hall
The Shifting Hall is the mobile capital of the Veilkin Masquerade. Its changing location reflects a society built around secrecy, fluid identity, espionage, and the deliberate denial of fixed political boundaries.

### Isenhall
Isenhall is the gathering place and capital of the Tribes, where orcish families and clans negotiate leadership and tradition. Its importance grows whenever the tribes must choose a warchief or respond to pressure from Iskandria and Arrkoth.

### Varkuun
Varkuun is a named settlement of the Tribes where orcish families gather around local chiefs, craft, and martial tradition. Its loyalties are shaped by the wider question of how the clans should choose and obey a warchief.

### Nattlys
Nattlys is a named settlement of the Tribes where night markets, clan halls, and watch fires serve travelers moving through the orcish territories. Its political role follows the wider tribal balance between local independence, warchief authority, and pressure from neighboring powers.

### Belyndor
Belyndor is a city of the Valdengard Territories. It belongs to the forest realm's network of communities whose relationship with Arrkoth remains shaped by dependence, resistance, and the protection of sacred places.

### Brynmarrow
Brynmarrow is a named settlement of the Valdengard Territories surrounded by forest cultures and non-human communities. Its relationship with Arrkoth is shaped by local autonomy, sacred places, and the need to protect the living wilderness.

### Vinterhavn
Vinterhavn is the capital of the Vindrik Concord and a major harbor among the northern fjords. It is the political center of the elected jarls and the Concord's culture of sailors, clans, and skaldic historians.

### Eldborg
Eldborg is a named settlement of the Vindrik Concord. Its people share the Concord's northern traditions and must contend with the cold, difficult seas, and rival clan interests.

### Vargsholm
Vargsholm is a named settlement of the Vindrik Concord among fjords, runestones, and cold-water routes. Its people share the Concord's traditions of clan politics, sailing, and oral history.

### Stalgrad
Stalgrad is a named settlement of the Vindrik Concord. Its life is governed by the northern alliance of jarls, sailors, and historians who preserve their independence against the cold and against one another.



### The Sapphire Academy of Theoretical Magic
The Sapphire Academy is Al'Zafira's center for theoretical magic, Labyrinth studies, and controlled experimentation. Its public districts include lecture halls, observatories, archives, gardens, and laboratories, while its sandstone crypts hold more restricted work.

### Meridian
Meridian is a secured Kharaki research facility connected to the Academy and the city's underground systems. Its elevators, teleportation pillars, ventilation routes, and library guardians make it as difficult to escape as it is to study.

### Tsathoggua's Hole
Tsathoggua's Hole is a sealed subterranean chamber beneath Al'Zafira containing an ancient and dangerous presence. Kharaki researchers use silence, rituals, and strict procedures to prevent its occupant from waking.

### Fatamorgana
Fatamorgana is an exclusive pleasure house in Al'Zafira built around private salons, gardens, baths, and elaborate security. Beneath its polished hospitality, it serves as a meeting place for wealthy clients, officials, researchers, and people who need to disappear.

### The Al'Zafira Undercity
The Al'Zafira undercity is a network of slums, service passages, wells, hidden businesses, and government installations beneath the oasis city. It is where official authority, trafficking, espionage, and experimental science most visibly overlap.

### The Kharaki Government Laboratory
The Kharaki government laboratory is a concealed research complex beneath Al'Zafira used for biological, magical, and military projects. Its work includes altered soldiers, Labyrinth research, and the containment of people whose knowledge is considered strategically valuable.

### Mir'ah
Mir'ah is the river and water network that runs through Al'Zafira. Its wells, channels, and underground reservoirs are essential to the city's survival and become vulnerable whenever political or magical conflict reaches the streets.

### The de Venaris Laboratory
The de Venaris laboratory is Nivara's private research site for studying the Bloom, corrupted organisms, and possible cures. It contains evidence of both compassionate medical goals and experiments whose ethics are much harder to defend.

### The Darne Household
The Darne household is an old family residence tied to artificial life, Grimwald Mirrors, and the lost memories of the Porcelain Lady. Its records may connect a respectable lineage to experiments that were never meant to be remembered.

### JPL Typography
JPL Typography is a brick, palazzo-style industrial estate in Korso containing the printing works, administration, and Lucrece's household. Its public business produces newspapers, while its private spaces have become associated with exotic collections, missing workers, and strange machinery.

### The JPL New Press
The JPL New Press is the functioning part of the Korso printing operation. Its workers produce the city's newspapers while growing increasingly secretive and uneasy about what is happening elsewhere on the estate.

### The JPL Old Press
The Old Press is a locked and largely abandoned printing building where machines continue to operate after dark. Its animated gargoyles and unexplained output make it one of the clearest signs that JPL is being used for more than publishing.

### The JPL Household
The JPL Household is Jean-Pierre Lucrece's mansion and exhibition space, with gardens, statues, animal enclosures, private rooms, and damaged underground wings. Once a display of luxury and exotic life, it now bears the marks of escape, destruction, and experiments involving R'auleks.

### Father Maxim's Church
Father Maxim's Church is a large, decaying gothic cathedral in Korso. It remains an important center of Imperial worship even as its authority clashes with local devotion to the Porcelain Lady and with the needs of the city's ordinary people.

### The Grotto
The Grotto is a hidden cavern and shrine outside Korso associated with the White Witch and the Porcelain Lady. It serves as a refuge, a place of pilgrimage, and the site of confrontations between villagers, Maer representatives, and those seeking to control the automaton.

### The Emporium
The Emporium is a Korso establishment marked by a large glass-encased clock in its center. Its public commerce provides a respectable face for a city where rare goods, rumors, and dangerous specimens often move through the same streets.

### The Fisherman's Hut
The Fisherman's Hut is the home of Alžbeta and her family near Korso's waterfront. It is a modest domestic landmark that shows how the city's religious and supernatural conflicts reach beyond laboratories and wealthy estates.

### The House of Death
The House of Death, also called the Domain of Death, is the institution and realm responsible for receiving, recording, and guiding the dead. Its hierarchy includes the Hanged Man, the Majordomus, Cardinals, the Commander of Decay, Stonemasons, Lightkeepers, Children of Death, and priests of the Hemp Rope.

### The Domain of Judgement
The Domain of Judgement is a neighboring divine realm connected to Abaddon and to Virgil d'Armenty's rebellion against the existing order. Its relationship with the House of Death is political, theological, and increasingly threatened by attempts to redirect souls between domains.

### The Throne of Bones
The Throne of Bones is the seat of authority within the House of Death. Whoever controls it can influence the laws, rituals, and balance that govern the passage between life and death.

### The Gallows
The Gallows is the Hanged Man's personal retreat within the Domain of Death. It is both a symbol of his office and a place where private memory, judgment, and the history of his apotheosis converge.

### The Hemp Abbey
The Hemp Abbey is the center of the priests of the Hemp Rope and the place where the sacred hemp is cultivated. Its priests serve as healers, investigators, and guardians of the doctrine that death must not be overturned without cause.

### The Tower of Memories
The Tower of Memories is an archive where the memories of the dead are written into stone. Its records are among the House's greatest sources of authority and among the most dangerous things that can be stolen, altered, or withheld.

### The Stone Graveyard
The Stone Graveyard holds statues depicting the deceased, created by the Stonemason and their apprentices. It is a place of remembrance, but also a reminder that the House of Death preserves faces and identities as carefully as it preserves souls.

### The Soulwell
The Soulwell is a vast abyss where souls are gathered before their final passage. Its depth and origin are among the House of Death's oldest unanswered questions, and several factions believe that something far older waits below.

### Glitnirdur
Glitnirdur is an old settlement connected to the origins of the Butterfly, the Black Sepsis, and the strange trade in jewels and life crystals. Its history links the mudlands, the House of Death, and the ancient experiments that produced new forms of immortal life.

### Avanca
Avanca is a place where Garrim encountered a brilliant and unstable inventor whose work may connect ordinary craft to the wider crisis around the House of Death. It is remembered as a meeting point for eccentric minds, unfinished machines, and dangerous ideas.

### Silvaruna
Silvaruna is a forest where the Hanged Man investigated a murdered dryad and encountered traces that vanished into the Labyrinth. Its woods are associated with old nature worship, missing souls, and the fragile boundary between the living world and divine domains.

### Summit
Summit is an ancient elven metropolis now connected to the Dreamlands and the inner regions of Glitnirdur. Its surviving ruins and archives preserve memories of the elven departure, the Grimwald Mirrors, and the civilizations that existed before the present powers.

### Maer Ruins
The Maer Ruins are the surviving remnants of a civilization whose representatives seek to recover their dead and restore their former power. Their mountains, dragon-bound relics, life crystals, and buried archives remain central to the struggle over Balam's Iscalia and the lost Maer legacy.

### The Maer Mountains
The Maer Mountains contain old ruins, life-crystal deposits, and remnants of the civilization's dragon-bound legacy. They are central to competing plans to resurrect the Maer dead or break the bond between Maer souls and their former rulers.

### The Domain of Abaddon
The Domain of Abaddon is a divine realm reached through old Maer rites and connected to protection, justice, and living order. Its involvement in the R'aulek project shows how divine powers can become entangled with experiments that began as political or scientific undertakings.

### The Underbelly
The Underbelly is the pulsing interior region of the Lady's infected body, where travelers move through living tissue rather than ordinary stone or soil. It contains settlements, predators, memories, and paths that change as the body responds to the Bloom.

### The Rot
The Rot is the corpse-filled outer region of the Lady's body and one of the first major barriers faced by travelers entering her interior. Decaying bodies, parasites, scavengers, and infected creatures make it a landscape where the ground itself may move.

### The Shallows
The Shallows are a comparatively less infected stretch of the Lady's interior. They provide a brief respite before the deeper regions, but even here travelers remain isolated from the normal world and vulnerable to the body's changing geography.

### The Mouth
The Mouth is a vertical passage through the Lady's flesh where magic weakens and ordinary navigation fails. Its walls contain alcoves, vines, and routes toward the Ossuary and deeper organs.

### The Ossuary
The Ossuary is a sacred and dangerous chamber inside the Lady, centered on an effigy associated with her worship. Petrified remains, falling water, and visions of the Plague King make it a place of both pilgrimage and corruption.

### Bile Lake
Bile Lake is a corrosive body of yellow-green fluid inside the Lady. An obelisk lies beneath its surface, and the lake's poisonous waters make even a short crossing a serious hazard.

### The Carnivorous Grove
The Carnivorous Grove is a region of predatory ferns, hidden sinkholes, and dangerous wildlife within the Lady. It is beautiful at a distance and lethal up close, especially to anyone who mistakes stillness for safety.

### The Depths
The Depths are the least understood passages beneath the Lady's more familiar interior regions. They contain older ruins, hostile creatures, and routes toward the Membrane and the Core.

### The Settlement
The Settlement is one of the communities built inside the Lady by survivors, priests, researchers, and people who can no longer return to the surface. Its inhabitants depend on fragile protections while searching for food, cures, and a way out.

### The Membrane
The Membrane is a defensive boundary maintained by the Lady's priests to slow the spread of the Bloom. It separates surviving communities from the most heavily infected regions, although its protection is weakening.

### The Core
The Core is the deepest named region of the Lady's body and the destination of the journey through her infected interior. It is where Uriel's ritual threatens the living heart of the Lady and where the campaign's biological, divine, and personal conflicts converge.

### Gildore
Gildore is a large settlement within the Lady, built around a marble cathedral and illuminated by bioluminescent lamps. It was once a center of priests, nature shamans, scholars, and treasure hunters before the Bloom fractured its community.

### New Gilmore
New Gilmore is a settlement on the safer edge of the Lady's interior, known to travelers who have ventured far from the surface. It serves as a point of comparison for the older communities and abandoned routes deeper within the body.

### Corazón
Corazón is a city of the Golden Republic known for its distinctive ceramics and mercantile culture. It is also part of the Porcelain Lady's history through Mariona Mayólica, one of the students she rescued and educated.

### Santa Velory
Santa Velory is a coastal region remembered in the Porcelain Lady's recollections as a place where students from wealthy families and poor communities studied under her influence. Its history connects Korso's later industry to older networks of education and migration.
