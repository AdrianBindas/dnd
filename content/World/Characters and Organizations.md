## The Heroes of the Party
### Veyric de Venaris
Veyric is a kenku aristocrat, alchemist, and hunter of aberrant creatures. During the Third Omen he ascended upon the heaven to protect the world from Malady.
### Zaine Eshtiaq
Zaine is an operative connected to the Verdugo Syndicate and its artifact-recovery work. He is one of the party's main investigators of the Syndicate, the Imperial Church, and the strange mirror encountered during their missions.
### Stará Zverina
Zverina is a healer and alchemist who treats disease, poison, and magical contamination. She brings medical knowledge, aristocratic contacts, and a practical concern for the people affected by the party's investigations.
## Temporary Members of the Party
### Irek Dowd
Irek is a scholar and adventurer whose interests include rare books, artifacts, and stolen memories. He traveled with the party during the early Redguard investigation before leaving to pursue his own path.

## Ellyria
### Ukan
Ukan is a dwarf innkeeper who runs Door 78, a discreet meeting place connected to tunnels and hidden doors. She knows the city's rogues, thieves, and travelers better than most official informants.
### Sera Voss

Sera is an artificer who builds specialized prostheses in Al'Zafira. She assisted Zaine with a replacement hand and knew damaging information about the Verdugo succession.
## Verdugo's Syndicate (former)

### Ismael Francisco Verdugo
Ismael was the head of the Verdugo Syndicate, a network of factories, banks, shipping firms, and artifact operations. He recruited the party to investigate a Syndicate traitor and the Imperial Church's involvement in dangerous research. Adopted and manipulated Zaine Eshtiaq, who ultimately made sure Verdugo will not use him again and killed him in his own office.

### Santiago Laurens
Santiago leads the Syndicate's Arcane Containment Bureau and its militant interests. He is cautious around Zaine and treats the Syndicate's security, artifacts, and succession as matters of public danger.

### Nazar “Dynamite” Dyres
Dynamite is a university lecturer and Syndicate representative responsible for research and development. He is intelligent, volatile, and willing to support the party when scientific freedom and Syndicate interests align.

### Marcelle Edith
Marcelle represents banking and aristocratic finance within the Syndicate. She evaluates candidates and alliances through family reputation, status, and visible support from the upper classes.

### Prada
Prada represents gambling, distilleries, and parts of the Syndicate's illegal business. She is quiet and pragmatic, and she judges people by their close relationships rather than by rank alone.

### Riccard Donato
Riccard Donato controls a large share of the Syndicate's businesses and industrial companies. He is impatient, ambitious, and willing to use economic pressure against rivals and workers.

### Antonio Honorbrow
Antonio is a merchant and a more principled Syndicate representative. He opposes cooperation with murderers and wants the organization to abandon at least some of its illegal trade and artifact hoarding.
## Imperial Bureau
### Iskra
Iskra is a senior imperial official and trusted agent of the Empress. She tests the loyalty of her contractors and provides gave them access to imperial resources and identities.
### Chantal Anne-Marie
Chantal is an Imperial squad bard and informant. She supplied the party with intelligence, transport, and warnings about the Blind Messengers and the crisis in Redguard.
### Kabál
Kabál is an Imperial mage attached to Chantal's squad. He helped respond to the Loth Rakk crisis and used his remaining power to get survivors out of the collapsing complex.

## Redguard

### The Duke of Redguard
The Duke rules a fortified frontier duchy where imperial authority, separatists, forest peoples, and supernatural threats collide. The party met him during his court, festival, hunt, and the investigation surrounding the meteorite and Loth Rakk.
### Aldric Headstrong
Aldric owns docks and property in Redguard and presents himself as a respectable aristocrat. The party discovered his cooperation with a conspiracy against the Duke and defeated him in the investigation of Highwater.
### Elijah Bjornsson
Elijah is a local aristocrat, prison administrator, and tavern owner associated with the Redguard conspiracy. He supplied prisoners and local forces to Lord Dunhart while publicly presenting himself as a regional authority.
### Lord Dunhart
Dunhart was an aristocratic collector whose ship, the _Loyalty_, contained aberrant specimens, research notes, and evidence of coercive experiments. The party investigated the ship and connected his work to the meteorite, the Blind Messengers, and several powerful buyers.
### Lady Corvessa Dunhart
Corvessa is Dunhart's politically connected wife and a member of the Eternal Court. The party encountered her during the train operation and later received messages from her concerning Kane Keaton.
### Ichor
Ichor is the Duke's astronomer, whose observatory was found empty after he disappeared. His records connected the meteorite to unusual celestial activity and helped direct the party toward Loth Rakk.
### Grimoald Rutherford
Grimoald was an Imperial archbishop and lead researcher at Loth Rakk. The party found him responsible for a dangerous research complex whose experiments involved the meteorite, dimensional breaches, and aberrant organisms.
### Wilhelm Rutherford
Wilhelm is Grimoald's son and a researcher at Loth Rakk. He worked directly with experimental subjects and represented the complex's scientific staff during the party's investigation.
## Caelith
### Thiala Duskstar
Thiala is an elven academic traveling with a restricted artifact and information from a university. The party met her on the Imperial train and became involved in the struggle over her cargo.
## Vesper
## Al'Zafira
### Kane Keaton
Kane is a former Syndicate associate accused of embezzling money and betraying the organization. The party investigated him in Redguard and Al'Zafira, where his connection to Nivara became part of a larger abduction and Kharaki research case.
### Nivara de Venaris
Nivara is a kenku alchemist from Veyric's family line. The party learned that she investigated the Bloom and the Plague King, was targeted by the Blind Messengers and Kharak, and disappeared under circumstances first reported as a murder.
### Azra Taher
Azra is an academic associated with the Sapphire Academy and research into Tsathoggua. The party encountered evidence that she had information about Nivara and was trying to conceal her involvement.

### Primus

Primus is a healer and religious figure the party met during the Absolution Square prelude. He was involved in treating people affected by demonic influence and the violence spreading through Ellyria's lower districts.

### Rosalinda

Rosalinda is an elderly trader who sold the party Kempel's Gas and a Gem of Seeing before the Redguard hunt. Her unusual requests and bird companions made her a continuing source of unanswered questions for Zverina.

### Khalid ibn Faruq

Khalid is a Vesperian traveler who competed in the Redguard festival's drinking contest. His use of blood-based preparations made him memorable to Zverina and connected ordinary festivities to unfamiliar regional practices.

### Lena

Lena is an enemy of the party who pursued them after the events involving Stella and the Redguard conspiracy. Her motives came from the loss and reanimation of someone close to her.

### Stella

Stella was a member of the Redguard conspiracy who was captured and questioned by the party. Her testimony exposed Dunhart's alliances and the existence of other groups interested in the Duke and the meteorite.

## Death and imperial history

### Ehaageris, the Hanged Man

Ehaageris is the god of death and the afterlife whose House receives and records the dead. The party learned of his disappearance through Sussane, the death cult, and the investigation into powers competing for the vacant authority.

### Crassus Arrkoth

Crassus is the founder of the Arrkoth Empire and a recurring figure in imperial and death-related prophecies. The party learned that his legacy is connected to the Empire's religious authority and the struggle around the Hanged Man.

## Ascendants and spirits
### Uriel Cabeth

Uriel is an occult leader associated with the Blind Messengers and the Bloom. The party encountered him at Loth Rakk and Redguard and learned that he could control people, cross dimensional boundaries, and direct the cult's larger threat.

### Sussane
Sussane is a mysterious child connected to the House of Death and the search for Uriel. She travels with Onyx and gave the party information about death, the Plague King, and the danger surrounding Nivara.

### Onyx

Onyx is Sussane's monstrous wolf-like guardian. It protects Sussane and has intervened during several encounters involving Uriel and the House of Death.
